Nov 27, 2026 Friday

  • 19:00 KST Daily China AI Daily Report · November 27 A State Council executive meeting at midday, with its reference to a 'stabilisation fund' role for Central Huijin, turned the whole A-share market around. Shanghai rose 3.73% to 3,606.49 and reclaimed 3,600; ChiNext jumped 11.61% and the STAR 50 15.07%, the largest ChiNext day since 8 October 2024 and the STAR 50's third-best on record. Turnover rose CNY 682bn to CNY 1.9998tn, with 5,382 stocks up against 181 down. All eleven of our names rose: Zhongji Innolight +19.98%, limit-ups for Eoptolink, Piotech, VeriSilicon, Shengyi and GigaDevice, Cambricon +18.68%, Hygon +18.05%, Montage +15.69%, SMIC +15.38% and CXMT +12.97%. Zhongji Innolight led the market on both turnover (CNY 33.397bn) and main-fund inflow (CNY 3.769bn). 40 sources ↗
  • 20:00 KST Spotlight China AI Spotlight · Piotech — how the thin-film leader earns its keep, with domestic tools past 50% Third-quarter revenue of CNY 2.308bn was a quarterly record and non-GAAP net profit of CNY 561m more than doubled from the second quarter. Gross margin reached 42.47%, up from 35.0% for all of 2025. The headline CNY 2.431bn of nine-month net profit is mostly a CNY 1.616bn fair-value gain on listed stakes; the operating business doubled on a non-GAAP basis. Today's limit-up was the sector, not the company: reports that domestic tools took more than half of purchases on the third-phase line of a leading memory maker, SEMI's USD 149bn 2026 front-end forecast and China's first equipment quality standard all landed on the same day. The problem is the price: 57 times trailing earnings and about 2% below the highest analyst target. 25 sources ↗

Nov 26, 2026 Thursday

  • 19:00 KST Daily China AI Daily Report · November 26 The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell more than 7% overnight before closing down 0.65%, and a new government plan for next-generation networks put 1.6T optical transport and 6G at the centre of policy, moving money out of chipmaking and into optical modules and PCBs. Shanghai rose 0.01% to 3,476.88, its first gain in two sessions, but it could not reclaim 3,500; the STAR 50 fell another 1.36%. Three of our eleven names advanced: Zhongji Innolight +5.18%, Eoptolink +4.26% and Shengyi +5.85%. Zhongji Innolight led the market on both turnover (CNY 19.706bn) and main-fund inflow (CNY 1.690bn). CXMT fell 2.71% and was the market's largest target of main-fund selling at CNY 795m, while SMIC dropped 4.51%. The index was flat but 3,642 stocks fell. 29 sources ↗
  • 20:00 KST Spotlight China AI Spotlight · Eoptolink — a quarter lost to floods, a capacity surge held for Q4 Q3 revenue of CNY 10.940bn (+80.3%) and net profit of CNY 3.843bn (+61.2%) look like a stall until you see what caused them: flooding in Thailand pushed September shipments into October. The company has grown production capacity 1.9x in a year but could only run 38% of it because materials were short. Q4 is where that slack turns into revenue. We take apart the numbers of a stock that rose 4.26% today while chips fell — 97.92% overseas revenue, a concentrated customer base, tariffs and the yuan included. 23 sources ↗

Nov 25, 2026 Wednesday

  • 19:00 KST Daily China AI Daily Report · November 25 The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 7.88% overnight, Micron lost 11% and A-shares followed: Shanghai -2.03%, ChiNext -4.19%, STAR 50 -5.12%, with 3,500 and 1,200 both giving way. Optical modules went the other way. Beijing's suspension of part of its US dual-use export controls through late January pulled money into the export chain, leaving Eoptolink up 0.72% and Zhongji Innolight down just 1.92%, while TFC Optical jumped 7.65%. Eoptolink was the only gainer among our eleven names; CXMT fell 7.34% and was the market's largest target of main-fund selling. 26 sources ↗
  • 20:00 KST Spotlight China AI Spotlight · CXMT — how far does an 89.5% gross margin go? Q3 revenue of CNY 118.714bn (+613.2%), net profit of CNY 54.435bn and an 89.53% gross margin — the highest in the industry, above Micron's 86.8% and SK hynix's 86.0%. Yet the stock fell 7.34% on the day and was the market's largest target of main-fund selling. In four months since listing, its market value has gone from CNY 3.28tn to CNY 2.2809tn. Here is what sits between a market selling the cycle and a company that has already sold its 2027 output. 26 sources ↗

Optical modules and PCB names gave back 8–10% from their intraday highs, while domestic chips such as Cambricon (+6.03%), SMIC (+4.04%) and Hua Hong (+10.36%) kept their gains to the close. 29 sources ↗
  • 20:00 KST Spotlight China AI Spotlight · Shengyi Technology — profit tripled, the share price halved Q3 revenue of CNY 14.182bn (+78.8%), net profit of CNY 3.44bn (+238.4%) and a 41.63% gross margin — all records. Yet the stock opened limit-up and closed up just 1.40%, and the CNY 340bn market value of May is now CNY 149.6bn. Here is what sits between a material that keeps getting more expensive and the 2027 demand that has to pay for it. 17 sources ↗
