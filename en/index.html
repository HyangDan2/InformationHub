China AI Spotlight Nov 27, 2026 China AI Spotlight · Piotech — how the thin-film leader earns its keep, with domestic tools past 50% Third-quarter revenue of CNY 2.308bn was a quarterly record and non-GAAP net profit of CNY 561m more than doubled from the second quarter. Gross margin reached 42.47%, up from 35.0% for all of 2025.
