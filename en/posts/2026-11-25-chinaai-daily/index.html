China AI Daily Report · November 25

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 7.88% overnight, Micron lost 11% and A-shares followed: Shanghai -2.03%, ChiNext -4.19%, STAR 50 -5.12%, with 3,500 and 1,200 both giving way. Optical modules went the other way. Beijing's suspension of part of its US dual-use export controls through late January pulled money into the export chain, leaving Eoptolink up 0.72% and Zhongji Innolight down just 1.92%, while TFC Optical jumped 7.65%. Eoptolink was the only gainer among our eleven names; CXMT fell 7.34% and was the market's largest target of main-fund selling.

Including the Beijing exchange the total was CNY 1.3927tn (-CNY 275.1bn). Only five stocks traded more than CNY 10bn: Zhongji Innolight (CNY 17.063bn) and Eoptolink (CNY 16.411bn) were first and second, and Cambricon was fifth at CNY 10.249bn.

The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,476.47 (-2.03%), losing 3,500. The Shenzhen Component fell to 10,086.36 (-3.24%), ChiNext to 2,183.58 (-4.19%), the STAR 50 to 1,186.14 (-5.12%), the STAR Composite to 1,317.53 (-5.93%) and the BSE 50 to 755.87 (-4.75%). ChiNext is at its lowest since July 2025 and has halved from its June 25 peak. There were 586 advancers against 4,926 decliners, 51 limit-ups and more than 280 stocks down 9% or more. Turnover was CNY 1.3831tn, down CNY 272bn on the day. The volume did not vanish; most of it was selling.

The trigger came from the US. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped 7.88%, with all thirty members lower. Nvidia gave back half of Monday's 31% gain, falling 5.86%, while Micron lost 10.97%, AMD 8.9% and TSMC 4%. Anthropic closing below its IPO price (-6.54%) and a spike in CoreWeave bond yields revived the argument about returns on AI capital spending. Asia followed: the KOSPI fell 6.16% and halted trading, with Samsung Electronics down 9.87%, and the Nikkei was off as much as 3.6% intraday. A December Fed hike priced at 98%, a 10-year Treasury near 4.9% and reports of an imminent tariff announcement covering dozens of economies added to the pressure.

There was a domestic layer too. On November 23 the US Bureau of Industry and Security added seven entities to its export-control list and restricted advanced AI chip access for data centres providing compute to Chinese customers. That helps import substitution in the medium term, but on the day the tightening itself pushed investors to reduce risk. Semiconductors fell 8.30%, lithography 7.78%, the HBM complex 8.08%, and equipment and materials names went with them: Hua Hong -13.89%, NAURA -9.55%, AMEC -7.46%, Hangzhou Changchuan -9.88%. Money went the other way, into influenza and artemisinin drugs, retail, banks, coal and baijiu. That is how 4,900 stocks fell while the index lost 2%.

The exception was optical modules. MOFCOM said it would suspend the second clause of its 2024 Notice No. 46 on dual-use exports to the US from November 28 through January 27, 2027. Read as a signal of easing, it drew buyers into the export chain tied to North American cloud customers, while the same reading weakened the case for urgent domestic substitution and added to selling in local chip names. TFC Optical rose 7.65% and was the market's largest main-fund inflow at CNY 1.086bn. Zhongji Innolight ran to CNY 429.42 (+3.0%) intraday before closing at CNY 409.00 (-1.92%), still with the largest turnover in the market. Eoptolink opened at CNY 175.10, touched CNY 188.56 and closed at CNY 181.30 (+0.72%) — the only gainer among the eleven.

Flows moved to defensives. Main funds sold CNY 48.968bn across the market, with CNY 14.499bn leaving the electronics sector alone, while retail, food and beverage and chemical pharma took money in. By stock, CXMT saw CNY 1.791bn of net main-fund selling, the most in the market, and GigaDevice was second at CNY 906m. On the buy side, TFC Optical was followed by Qingshan Paper, BOE and Kweichow Moutai. Policy is pulling the other way: the three stabilisation measures announced on November 22 — an 80% minimum margin ratio from November 30, lower trading fees and a planned market-maker system for ChiNext — were followed on November 25 by CNY 800bn of MLF. But margin balances have fallen from about CNY 3tn in June to below CNY 2.2tn on November 20, so the capacity policy creates will take time to turn into buying.

688256 · Cambricon

CNY 907.90 (-6.40%). It opened at 945.00 and held as high as 959.80 before sliding to 900.10 in the afternoon. Turnover was CNY 10.249bn, third among our names and fifth in the market. Main funds turned to a net outflow of CNY 836m after eight sessions of buying. There was no company news. The stock had ridden Monday's US AI chip rally and gave it back in one day as the SOX fell 7.88%. The medium-term case — domestic AI chip share above 50% and tighter BIS rules pushing demand inward — has not changed, but it carries little weight against a December hike and the AI capex debate. Structure and risks were covered in the November 20 spotlight → Cambricon spotlight.

688041 · Hygon

CNY 157.95 (-3.10%). It opened at 158.90, bounced to 163.40 and then slid to 156.50. This was the fifth straight decline, for a cumulative -16.47%. Turnover of CNY 3.766bn and a 1.02% turnover ratio made it the thinnest of the eleven. Thin names see supply on every bounce. On November 20 the company said its supply chain was stable, CPU and DCU demand was still growing and next-generation development was on schedule, but that did not turn the price. No further execution has been disclosed on its CNY 500–800m buyback since CNY 235m was spent on November 19. See the November 19 spotlight → Hygon spotlight.

300308 · Zhongji Innolight

CNY 409.00 (-1.92%). It gapped down to 401.00, ran to 429.42 (+3.0%) and settled at 409.00. On a day when the index lost 2% and semis lost 8%, that is resilience. Turnover of CNY 17.063bn was the largest in the market, with a 3.70% turnover ratio. MOFCOM's suspension of part of the US export controls worked as a positive for the export chain, and an Open Source Securities report arguing that CPO and NPO commercialisation is accelerating helped. Company news was limited to the CNY 2–4bn buyback at a CNY 600 cap announced on the night of November 23. The stock is 2% below Monday but still 71% below its June peak of CNY 1,415.68, at 18.6 times trailing earnings. The gap between the company's view and the market's was the subject of the November 23 spotlight → Zhongji Innolight spotlight.

300502 · Eoptolink

CNY 181.30 (+0.72%). It opened at 175.10 (-2.7%), reached 188.56 (+4.7%) and held a gain into the close — the only one of the eleven. Turnover of CNY 16.411bn ranked second in the market and its 7.19% turnover ratio was the highest in our group. There was no new filing. The November 18 statement that customer guidance is clear, backlog is sufficient and no orders have been cancelled, together with 1.6T volume production, remains the base, and on this day the US-China easing signal was added to it. Of the CNY 800m–1bn buyback approved in October, CNY 800m (4.216m shares at an average of CNY 189.7) had been executed by November 5. The close is still below that average, so whether the remaining programme supports the floor is the near-term question.

688981 · SMIC

CNY 122.00 (-3.56%). It opened at 123.92, bounced to 125.75 and then came within a hair of 120.00, closing near the low. Turnover was CNY 7.131bn with a 2.90% turnover ratio, and main funds were a net CNY 45m seller — flows were not the problem. H-shares (00981) fell more than 4% early before paring the loss. Q3 revenue of USD 3.19bn, a 29.9% gross margin and Q4 guidance of +4–6% at 26–28% were already known; on the day the "SMIC concept" basket fell 7.58% and dragged suppliers and equipment names with it. The tension between BIS rules narrowing overseas channels and pushing domestic design demand to domestic fabs is the same as on November 24.

600183 · Shengyi Technology

CNY 58.43 (-5.15%). It opened at 59.01 and traded up to 61.88 before sliding to 58.35 in the afternoon. Turnover of CNY 4.588bn was about half the previous day's CNY 7.913bn, with a 3.18% turnover ratio. There was no company-specific bad news. A stock that opened limit-up and closed up 1.40% on Monday was already carrying profit-taking supply, and on this day PCB and CCL names followed AI hardware lower. The numbers stand: Q3 revenue of CNY 14.182bn (+78.8%), net profit of CNY 3.440bn (+238.4%) and a 41.63% gross margin. The market cap is CNY 141.9bn at 18.6 times trailing earnings, still far below May's CNY 340bn. The relationship between price and profit was covered on November 24 → Shengyi spotlight.

688072 · Piotech

CNY 466.66 (-9.80%). Its open of 504.04 was the high, and it fell to 463.15, one of the sharpest drops among equipment names. Turnover was CNY 3.059bn with a 2.23% turnover ratio. NAURA fell 9.55%, Hangzhou Changchuan 9.88%, Fortune Precision 13.09% and ACM Research Shanghai 7.30% as equipment and components sold off together. This group has been among the year's biggest winners, so it gives back the most when rates and capex expectations wobble. Company news is limited to the November 20 approval of its CNY 2.5bn acquisition of Wuxi Shangji Semiconductor and a proposed second buyback of CNY 200–300m. Nine-month revenue was CNY 5.220bn (+23.70%) and adjusted net profit CNY 928m (+102.66%), with profit still outgrowing revenue.

603986 · GigaDevice

CNY 198.28 (-6.69%). It opened at 205.60, traded up to 210.98 and then closed at the day's low. Turnover was CNY 5.836bn with a 4.27% turnover ratio. Main funds sold CNY 906m net, second only to CXMT. The stock had run 6.7% on Monday's memory shortage reports, so profit-taking was heavy, and TrendForce's cut to 2027 forecasts plus Micron's 11% fall added to it. On the company side, it opened an automotive chip technology lab with Desay SV on November 24. Q3 revenue of CNY 8.732bn (+225.65%) and net profit of CNY 4.209bn (+728.93%) already reflect memory pricing, and by November 21 it had bought back 3.8387m shares for CNY 1.310bn. Market cap is CNY 139.6bn at 12.0 times trailing earnings.

688008 · Montage Technology

CNY 126.40 (-5.08%). It opened at 128.88, reached 130.57 and then fell to 126.17. Turnover was CNY 4.063bn with a 2.77% turnover ratio, and main funds were roughly flat with a net CNY 120m outflow. Memory interface chips track server shipments, so they fall with server and storage names on days like this. The company's own news remains supportive: nine-month revenue of CNY 5.414bn (+33.4%) and net profit of CNY 3.237bn (+98.3%), with CNY 2.50–2.79bn of interconnect orders booked as of October 26 for fourth-quarter delivery. It is shipping fifth-generation DDR5 RCD and DDR5-9200 CKD chips, has passed PCIe 6.x/CXL 3.x retimer certification and is sampling its CXL 3.2 MXC. The expanded AMD 6th-gen EPYC work will show up in next quarter's volumes. Market cap is CNY 154.3bn.

688521 · VeriSilicon

CNY 75.55 (-9.15%). It opened at 79.59, traded up to 82.11 and fell to 75.49 in the afternoon. Turnover of CNY 1.801bn and a 4.35% turnover ratio made it the least traded of the eleven; thin names move like this when they break. There was no filing. The stock had lost 31% last week and bounced 4.50% on Monday, and gave all of it back as domestic AI chip and NPU names fell 8–11%. The medium-term story — design IP licensing and chiplet integration — is unchanged; the checks are order momentum from the Q3 report and deeper work with domestic fabs and packaging houses. Market cap is CNY 39.7bn and the company is still loss-making.

688825 · CXMT

CNY 33.60 (-7.34%). It opened at 35.00, peaked at 35.38 and slid to 33.50. Turnover was CNY 9.483bn with a 6.13% turnover ratio. Flows were the worst in our group: main funds sold CNY 1.791bn net, the largest of any stock in the market and about 12% of the day's electronics outflow. Three things drove it: Micron's 10.97% fall overnight, TrendForce's cut to 2027 memory forecasts, and the weaker domestic-substitution premium implied by MOFCOM's suspension. The fundamentals point the other way. Q3 revenue was CNY 118.714bn (+613.2%), net profit CNY 54.435bn and gross margin 89.53%, above Micron's 86.8% and SK hynix's 86.0%. On November 19 it raised its buyback to CNY 2.5–5.0bn at a CNY 63.78 cap, subject to a December 9 shareholder vote. With only 6.6% of shares in free float, moves are amplified. Today's spotlight covers the company in depth → CXMT spotlight.

What to watch

  1. 01
    Policy

    The export-control suspension runs only to January 27

    MOFCOM suspended the second clause of its 2024 Notice No. 46 on dual-use exports to the US from November 28 through January 27, 2027. That single move sent optical modules higher in an otherwise brutal session for chips. But two months is short. Unless the suspension is extended or tariff talks move forward, the premium now priced into the export chain can come out again. On the same day, Washington's BIS added seven entities on November 23 and tightened access to advanced AI chips for data centres serving Chinese customers.

    January 27, 2027
  2. 02
    Rates

    A December hike is 98% priced; the 10-year sits near 4.9%

    After the New York Fed president kept consecutive hikes on the table, the odds of a December increase rose to 98% and the US 10-year stayed around 4.9%. Higher discount rates take the most out of long-duration growth stocks. That is part of why A-share semis fell more than twice as much as the index. Inflation prints and Fed speakers before the December meeting set the next direction.

    December 8–9
  3. 03
    Memory

    TrendForce cut its 2027 memory outlook

    On November 25 TrendForce lowered its 2027 revenue forecast to USD 419.3bn for NAND and USD 1.15tn for DRAM, reflecting Chinese cloud providers' order cuts. NAND is expected to tip into a price decline as supply grows, while DRAM stays undersupplied but with smaller quarterly contract increases that level off in the second half of 2027. NAND spot prices are down about 20% since mid-October. December contract talks will confirm or overturn that picture.

    December contract talks
  4. 04
    Flows

    Turnover CNY 1.38tn and margin debt below CNY 2.2tn

    Turnover fell CNY 272bn from the previous day to CNY 1.3831tn. Margin balances have dropped from about CNY 3tn in June to below CNY 2.2tn on November 20, and on November 19 turnover hit a 16-month low of CNY 1.31tn. For the index to rebound, turnover and margin balances have to turn before price does. The first test is the count of new margin accounts in the week after the 80% minimum-margin rule takes effect on November 30.

    November 30 – early December
  5. 05
    Policy

    80% margin and CNY 800bn of MLF

    Of the three stabilisation measures announced on November 22 by the CSRC and the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing exchanges, the cut in the minimum margin ratio from 100% to 80% takes effect on November 30. The same day the PBOC injected CNY 800bn through its medium-term lending facility. Policy is putting a floor under liquidity, but whether that money rotates back into growth stocks is a separate question. ChiNext's planned market-maker system is the next item to watch.

    November 30 – December
  6. 06
    Earnings

    Q4 guidance and 2027 pricing

    CXMT holds an extraordinary shareholder meeting on December 9 to vote again on its CNY 2.5–5.0bn buyback. Chip companies report Q4 results in January, and most 2027 volumes and prices will be settled before that. SK hynix and Micron say much of 2027 is already sold, and Samsung's 2027–2029 capacity is reported to be largely contracted. This is a market checking volumes, not just prices.

    December – January 2027
