The STAR 50 closed +2.19% higher at 1,247.99. The Shanghai Composite (3,587.83, +0.09%), Shenzhen Component (10,659.41, +0.20%) and ChiNext (2,311.62, +0.85%) all rose too, but only modestly. Turnover across the market was CNY 1.38tn, CNY 87.5bn less than the previous day, and more than 4,100 names fell on a day when the indices were up. The gains were made by a small group of large technology stocks.

Overnight, Nvidia's third-quarter revenue came in at USD 115.8bn, ahead of the USD 109.3bn consensus. But with the US regulatory stance on China-bound data centres and optical transceivers unchanged, buying concentrated clearly in domestic AI chips, equipment and compute infrastructure. By sector, compute (算力) leasing, electronic components, telecom services, CPO and the Nvidia theme were among the biggest gainers, while film and theatres, precious metals, gaming and healthcare services fell.

On policy and industry, Guangdong province's Digital Guangdong 15th Five-Year Plan and reports of a 10–20% foundry price increase for 2027 supported expectations for domestic substitution.

Closing prices below are from the regular session on November 18. Where no separate filing or report could be confirmed for a name, the note says so.

688256 · Cambricon

As the bellwether for domestic compute chips it traded up to CNY 972.00 intraday and closed at CNY 949.00 (+4.89%). This is the first place demand shifts to when regulation tightens. Volume was 12.02m shares.

688041 · Hygon

CNY 189.10 (+3.59%). It traded between a high of CNY 194.30 and a low of CNY 182.01. The DCU and server CPU lines moved as one axis of the domestic compute-infrastructure replacement. After the close, the "Samsung foundry audit" rumour began to spread — the cause of the following day's volatility.

300308 · Zhongji Innolight

It shook off a weak morning and rebounded in the afternoon to close at CNY 396.30 (+0.84%). The company cited having secured most of its major customers' 2027 orders and received 2028 guidelines, 1.6T mass production, and a plan for NPO to open up in the second half of 2027.

300502 · Eoptolink

It rebounded to CNY 163.35 intraday but the close slipped to CNY 158.21 (−1.12%). Its CNY 800m buyback and 1.6T capacity expansion were noted, but not enough to overcome worries about optical-transceiver regulation.

688981 · SMIC

A soft CNY 115.10 (+1.45%). Expectations of front-end equipment demand from Yangtze Memory and ChangXin Memory capacity expansion attached more strongly to the equipment names than to SMIC itself.

600183 · Shengyi Technology

CNY 60.90 (+3.91%). Subsidiary Shengyi Electronics (688183) closed 20% limit-up, and a filing confirming completion of a shareholder increase came out the same day. A subsidiary story rather than the parent's CCL and PCB materials business lifted the stock.

688072 · Piotech

CNY 522.68 (+4.56%). Several reports pointed to front-end equipment demand from Yangtze and ChangXin Memory expansion (including ChangXin's new CNY 13bn R&D programme). No company-specific filing was found for the day.

603986 · GigaDevice

CNY 219.56 (+2.33%). Details of the previous day's earnings call were published: the outlook for a continuing NOR and SLC NAND shortage, and the expectation that edge-AI customised memory (phones, PCs, robots) at subsidiary Qingyun (青耘) will grow strongly in 2027.

688008 · Montage Technology

CNY 137.79 (+5.51%). Fourth-quarter interconnect chip orders due for delivery were guided at CNY 2.5–2.79bn (DDR5-centric), and expectations built for DDR6, where each RDIMM carries two RCDs. It was the strongest of the eleven names on the day.

688521 · VeriSilicon

CNY 90.40 (−2.65%). No separate filing or development could be confirmed for the day. It fell along with the AI-chip pullback.

688825 · CXMT

CNY 36.91 (+1.99%). Reports that ChangXin Memory is making a new CNY 13bn R&D investment kept capacity-expansion expectations alive. Volume of 227.23m shares was the highest of the eleven names.

What to Watch Next

  1. 01
    Policy

    The shape of US regulation in practice

    The scope of enforcement on China-bound data centres and optical transceivers will change how visible orders are for the two optical-module names (Zhongji Innolight and Eoptolink). The new BIS rules taking effect on Nov 19 are the first test.

    Effective Nov 19
  2. 02
    Pricing

    Official confirmation of the 2027 foundry price increases

    If a 10–20% increase is confirmed, profit estimates for SMIC and front-end equipment (Piotech) move together. The question is whether customer notices or results guidance come first.

    December results season
  3. 03
    Policy

    Detailed implementation of Guangdong's Digital Guangdong 15th Five-Year Plan

    It needs to translate into orders for domestic equipment and components. Local budgets and tender notices are the real signal.

    Fourth quarter
  4. 04
    Flows

    Flows after Shengyi Electronics' limit-up

    If the gap with parent Shengyi Technology widens, there is a risk of a pullback. The rebound came after three consecutive sharp falls from Nov 13 to 17, so volatility is high.

    1–2 weeks
