China AI Daily Report · November 20

Shanghai fell 1.23% and the STAR Composite 5.23%, with 5,236 names down as semis and lithography equipment sold off. SMIC was the only riser in our eleven, up 2.63% and 17.87% over four sessions, while banks and dividend names lifted the SSE 50 to +0.45% late in the day.

Turnover is ranked by each name's own traded value. Four of the top five were optical modules, AI chips or foundry, yet money ended the day in defensives.

The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,533.79 (−1.23%), the Shenzhen Component at 10,256.39 (−3.09%) and ChiNext at 2,212.13 (−3.54%). The STAR 50 ended at 1,231.09 (−3.12%) and the STAR Composite at 1,349.18 (−5.23%). That the composite fell more than twice as much as the 50 says the selling was concentrated in small and mid-cap technology rather than the index heavyweights. Turnover across the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing markets was CNY 1.4311tn, CNY 116.5bn more than the previous day. Turnover rising on a day the indices lose more than 3% means more people were heading for the exit than looking for bargains. 5,236 names fell, the seventh consecutive session with more than 4,000 decliners.

The trigger came from the US overnight. The Dow (−2.20%), Nasdaq (−2.23%) and S&P 500 (−2.03%) all fell more than 2%, and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost 2.41%. Anthropic's record IPO opened 30% above its offer price and closed up 17.07%, but the momentum faded, and a short-seller report on AMD revived questions about circular deals and accounting across AI hardware. The heaviest weight, though, was the debate over peak AI capex. Alibaba management said its 20GW data-centre target and capex plan could be adjusted to market conditions, and capex growth at other large platforms such as Tencent showed signs of slowing. With Fed tightening expectations keeping the 10-year Treasury yield high, the long-duration growth trade was marked down again on discount rates. Asian markets fell 4–6% and weaker regional currencies added to the drain.

By sector, lithography equipment fell 7.79%, semiconductors 6.85% and memory chips 6.61%. Then, late in the session, a different kind of buyer appeared. China Construction Bank, ICBC, China Citic Bank and China Yangtze Power all set record highs, and the SSE 50 reversed from a 2% intraday loss to close +0.45%. More than CNY 24bn traded in the SSE 50 ETF (510050), beating the previous record of CNY 21.45bn set in January. Money leaving semiconductors did not all leave the market; some of it moved into banks and high-dividend names. The PCB theme moved on its own as well: Zhongjing Electronics hit its second straight limit-up, and Aohong Electronics and Chaosheng Electronics also closed limit-up.

One caution about the information flow. A screenshot circulated claiming that the head of Huatai Securities research had recommended liquidating A-shares; the broker issued a statement saying the remarks were not made by anyone at its research institute and were untrue. On days when the index swings this hard, unverified posts like that move real orders — the same pattern as the "Samsung foundry audit" rumour that knocked AI chips down 17% intraday on Nov 19.

In short, this was less an index decline than a reversal in crowded AI and semiconductor positioning plus a rotation into defensives. Ten of our eleven names fell; the one that rose was SMIC.

688256 · Cambricon

It rose to CNY 940.00 early, above the previous close, as Jon Peddie Research's Nov 19 note on third-quarter results and the supply chain was read as a buy case. In the afternoon, as semis sold off across the board, it fell to CNY 893.01 before closing at CNY 920.00 (−1.50%) — a loss in the low single digits on a day the STAR Composite fell 5.23%. Its CNY 9.472bn of turnover ranked seventh in the A-share market. The business, the numbers and the supply bottleneck are covered in depth in a separate spotlight → Cambricon spotlight.

688041 · Hygon

Even with the previous day's disclosure of its first buyback — 1.3767m shares for CNY 235m at CNY 162.69–174.99 — the stock could not escape the sector. It fell to CNY 161.00 intraday and closed at CNY 164.25 (−8.75%), at the bottom of the range the company itself paid. Between CNY 265m and CNY 565m of the CNY 500–800m programme is still unused, and the pace of the remaining purchases will set the near-term floor. The name was covered in the Nov 19 spotlight → Hygon spotlight.

300308 · Zhongji Innolight

CNY 393.20 (−3.72%). It rebounded to CNY 403.00 intraday before slipping back. Turnover of CNY 14.941bn kept it first in the whole A-share market. The chairman's CNY 2–4bn buyback proposal still stands, but that catalyst alone could not hold the whole optical-module sector up. Trading in this name now looks driven less by the buyback than by fourth-quarter module shipments and pricing.

300502 · Eoptolink

It rose to CNY 170.96 intraday, the first of the eleven to attempt a rebound, and closed at CNY 165.00 (−1.23%). Turnover of CNY 14.741bn ranked second. The company's official line — no demand cut, no production cut, and 1.6T modules in large-scale production — supported the downside. It also recovered more than 4% from its low of CNY 158.31 to the close.

688981 · SMIC

The only riser among the eleven. CNY 125.46 (+2.63%) was its fourth consecutive gain, +17.87% over the run, giving a market capitalisation of CNY 1.074tn. Turnover of CNY 8.828bn ranked eighth in the market. The point is that the foundry-substitution bid held on a day when design houses and accelerators were hit. Some commentary argues the BIS licensing rules for leading-edge foundry work actually underline how scarce domestic foundry capacity is. Four straight sessions of gains, however, also leave it stretched short term.

600183 · Shengyi Technology

CNY 58.37 (−3.18%). The PCB theme was strong — Zhongjing Electronics at a second limit-up, Aohong and Chaosheng at limit-up — but the CCL and PCB-materials heavyweight did not join in and absorbed profit-taking. No new filing was found for the day. The CNY 200–300m buyback begun on Oct 22 is still running, and industry talk of a 15% rise in CCL prices for M6 and below and 25% for adhesive sheets has not been confirmed by the company.

688072 · Piotech

CNY 528.00 (−3.56%), giving back part of Thursday's +4.75%. A filing said its purchase of a stake in Wuxi Shangji passed the Shanghai Stock Exchange's simplified review, a step towards completing its thin-film deposition line-up with PVD process technology. The domestic equipment order story is intact, but sector weakness was the bigger force today.

603986 · GigaDevice

CNY 205.88 (−5.91%). It tracked the memory-chip sector's 6.61% fall and closed essentially on its intraday low of CNY 205.77. No new company filing was found. The medium-term case — a continuing NOR and SLC NAND shortage plus expansion in edge-AI memory — is unchanged, but for now sector beta is setting the price.

688008 · Montage Technology

CNY 128.41 (−6.68%). It fell despite the previous day's completion of a CNY 500m buyback (3.3139m shares at an average of CNY 150.88). That average is about 18% above today's close, which shows how far the market price has moved from the company's own view of value. The operating story — fifth-generation DDR5 RCD in mass production and a rising share of DDR5 third- to fifth-generation shipments — is unchanged.

688521 · VeriSilicon

CNY 76.90 (−7.45%). It followed the broad de-rating of design houses and fell to CNY 75.90 intraday. No company-specific filing was found for the day. The Nano-XR wearable IP subsystem launched on Nov 19 is a medium-term design-win catalyst, but the share price is currently driven by sector flows.

688825 · CXMT

CNY 35.16 (−6.24%), closing at the intraday low. The board approved on Nov 19 a rise in the buyback ceiling from CNY 1–2bn to CNY 2.5–5bn, yet no rebound followed. The price cap of CNY 63.78 is 1.8 times the current price. The programme allocates CNY 0.5–1bn to employee incentives, CNY 0.5–1bn to share cancellation and CNY 1.5–3bn to supporting value and shareholder rights, and still needs shareholder approval. Turnover of CNY 9.717bn was the highest on the STAR Market and sixth across the A-share market. That the stock fell after a confirmed buyback plan suggests today's selling pressure overwhelmed stock-specific news.

What to Watch Next

  1. 01
    Flows

    How fast the AI mega-cap crowding unwinds

    Decliners again topped 5,236, the seventh straight session above 4,000. The index can bounce while breadth stays narrow. Watch whether the late money into the SSE 50 ETF was a one-day defensive trade or whether semiconductor money actually rotated, and check early next week's flows.

    Nov 23–25
  2. 02
    Earnings

    Alibaba and Tencent capex guidance

    Alibaba management saying its 20GW data-centre target and capex plan could be adjusted to market conditions was one leg of today's selloff. At the same time it said investment in domestic chips and its own supply chain would be more aggressive. Fourth-quarter guidance will show whether domestic accelerator and optical-module orders actually rise.

    December results season
  3. 03
    Filings

    CXMT's CNY 2.5–5bn buyback needs shareholder approval

    The board passed on Nov 19 a rise in the buyback ceiling from CNY 1–2bn to CNY 2.5–5bn. The price cap of CNY 63.78 is 1.8 times today's close of 35.16, which shows the gap between the company's view of value and the market's. Shareholder approval is still pending, and CNY 0.5–1bn of the programme is earmarked for share cancellation.

    Watch the shareholder-meeting notice
  4. 04
    Flows

    Hygon's remaining CNY 265–565m

    Of a CNY 500–800m programme, the company bought 1.3767m shares for CNY 235m on Nov 19 at CNY 162.69–174.99. Today's close of 164.25 sits at the bottom of that range. How quickly the rest is bought is the near-term gauge of downside support.

    Filings over the next 1–2 weeks
  5. 05
    Policy

    How hard the new BIS rules are enforced

    Rules covering remote access to AI chips from overseas data centres, intermediary-country exports and licensing for leading-edge foundry work took effect on Nov 19. They push domestic substitution demand up, but they also narrow the overseas routes available to design houses and foundries. The first licence decisions are the thing to watch.

    Weeks after implementation
  6. 06
    Risk

    Cambricon's inventory and cash flow

    Third-quarter operating cash flow was −CNY 83.68m, inventory at end-September was CNY 11.66bn and total liabilities reached CNY 7.236bn, up 56% from end-June. How much of the revenue and profit growth converts into cash is the key test next quarter, alongside whether the inventory is consumed by fourth-quarter sales and whether receivables days stretch.

    Fourth-quarter report
