China AI Daily Report · November 26

The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell more than 7% overnight before closing down 0.65%, and a new government plan for next-generation networks put 1.6T optical transport and 6G at the centre of policy, moving money out of chipmaking and into optical modules and PCBs. Shanghai rose 0.01% to 3,476.88, its first gain in two sessions, but it could not reclaim 3,500; the STAR 50 fell another 1.36%. Three of our eleven names advanced: Zhongji Innolight +5.18%, Eoptolink +4.26% and Shengyi +5.85%. Zhongji Innolight led the market on both turnover (CNY 19.706bn) and main-fund inflow (CNY 1.690bn). CXMT fell 2.71% and was the market's largest target of main-fund selling at CNY 795m, while SMIC dropped 4.51%. The index was flat but 3,642 stocks fell.

Including the Beijing exchange the total was CNY 1.326tn (-CNY 66.6bn). Only three stocks traded more than CNY 10bn: Zhongji Innolight (CNY 19.706bn) and Eoptolink (CNY 16.315bn) were first and second, and Cambricon was fifth at CNY 8.961bn. The third was ChiNext heavyweight CATL at CNY 13.156bn.

The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,476.88 (+0.01%), its first gain in two sessions, but it did not reclaim 3,500. The intraday range was 3,455.04 to 3,497.81, less than 34 points. The Shenzhen Component ended at 10,084.04 (-0.02%), ChiNext at 2,189.82 (+0.29%), the STAR 50 at 1,169.99 (-1.36%), the STAR Composite at 1,304.37 (-1.00%) and the BSE 50 at 752.44 (-0.45%). The index level hides the damage: 1,766 advancers against 3,642 decliners, with 68 limit-ups against roughly five limit-downs. Turnover was CNY 1.3174tn, down CNY 65.7bn and lower for a third session; including Beijing the total was CNY 1.326tn.

New York could not hold its bounce. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell more than 7% intraday before closing down 0.65%. Nvidia lost 4%, AMD 2% and Micron 4.5%, each down more than 8% at the worst point. October core PCE came in at 3.3% year on year against 3.2% expected, the highest since November 2023, and the odds of a December hike moved to 100%. The Nasdaq fell 0.62%, the S&P 500 0.25% and the Dow rose 0.33%. Asia went the other way: the KOSPI rose 0.29% to 4,711.21, clawing back part of Tuesday's 6.16% slide, with SK hynix up 2.23% and Samsung 0.35%. The Nikkei added 0.86%, and Kioxia jumped 12.57% after announcing work with Nvidia on using SSDs to expand GPU-accessible memory.

What moved A-shares was domestic. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Cyberspace Administration and the NDRC published an implementation plan for next-generation communications networks that names 400G/800G and 1.6T high-speed transport, 6G, ultra-high-speed optical links and ten-thousand-card cluster interconnection. Communications equipment rose 2.74% and money moved into CPO and optical names. Industry news added to it: the Global Electronics Association warned that the PCB shortage will last into the first half of 2027, and TrendForce expects global AI server shipments to grow 28.3% in 2026. Printed circuit boards rose 4.37% and components 3.94%, with Jinbaize, Jinlu Electronics, Junya, Chongda and Aohong limit-up or up more than 15%. Advanced packaging also ran after Huawei's five-layer hybrid-bonded HBM paper appeared on the ISSCC 2027 agenda: Shenghe Jingwei rose 18.94%, and Kuaike Intelligent, Sanjia and Dagang were limit-up.

Chips were sold. Semiconductors saw CNY 2.567bn of net main-fund outflow, with equipment and materials weakest: AMEC -6.52%, Hwatsing -5.94%, NAURA -3.14%. That is why the STAR 50 fell 1.36% while ChiNext rose. SMIC lost 4.51% and the SMIC concept basket 0.71%. Money went to components (CNY 4.527bn inflow), communications equipment (CNY 2.772bn) and precious metals (CNY 1.19bn), while batteries and brokers saw outflows. Precious metals rose 4.23% as spot gold recovered from USD 3,426 an ounce to USD 3,588. Banks made new highs — Chengdu, Bank of Communications, CITIC and Jiangsu — and coal and oil supported the index. Large caps held the index up while growth fell: a rotation day.

Three of our eleven names rose. Zhongji Innolight closed at CNY 430.18 (+5.18%) after touching 440.00, taking both the largest turnover in the market (CNY 19.706bn) and the largest main-fund inflow (CNY 1.690bn). Eoptolink followed at CNY 189.03 (+4.26%), and Shengyi Technology rose CNY 61.85 (+5.85%) with PCB and CCL names. Cambricon recovered CNY 942.85 (+3.85%), about half of Tuesday's loss. On the other side, CXMT fell to CNY 32.69 (-2.71%) with CNY 795m of net main-fund selling, the worst in the market for a second day, and SMIC dropped to CNY 116.50 (-4.51%) after touching 115.76. Those two and the equipment names explain why the STAR 50 fell while ChiNext rose.

688256 · Cambricon

CNY 942.85 (+3.85%). It opened at 920.00, ran to 956.58 and dipped to 888.20 before settling at 942.85. Turnover of CNY 8.961bn ranked third among our names and fifth in the market. Main funds turned to a net inflow of CNY 330m, a day after ending an eight-session buying streak. There was no company news. On a day when semiconductors saw CNY 2.567bn leave the sector, the domestic AI chip leader rose on its own. The backdrop is the BIS rules and notices from TSMC and Samsung suspending sub-16nm foundry work for mainland customers, which revives the case for domestic substitution. At 142 times trailing earnings and a CNY 592.9bn market cap, it remains exposed to the rate and capex debate. Structure and risks were covered in the November 20 spotlight → Cambricon spotlight.

688041 · Hygon

CNY 157.88 (-0.04%). It opened at 159.74, slid to 153.33 and closed almost exactly where it started. Turnover was CNY 3.211bn with a 0.88% turnover ratio, the thinnest of the eleven, and main funds sold CNY 103m net. From CNY 189.10 on November 18 to CNY 157.88 today the stock is down 16.5% in six sessions, five of them consecutive declines. It sits 13% below its 20-day average of 180.98. Nothing new has been disclosed since the November 20 statement that supply is stable, CPU and DCU demand is still growing and next-generation development is on schedule. No further execution on the CNY 500-800m buyback has been reported since CNY 235m was spent on November 19. Trailing P/E is 106.96 and the market cap is CNY 366.9bn. See the November 19 spotlight → Hygon spotlight.

300308 · Zhongji Innolight

CNY 430.18 (+5.18%). It opened at 417.94, ran to 440.00, dipped to 413.00 and closed at 430.18. Turnover of CNY 19.706bn was the largest in the market with a 4.17% turnover ratio, and main funds bought a net CNY 1.690bn, also the largest. There was no company filing. The driver was the three-ministry network plan naming 1.6T and 6G directly, with support from the CNY 2-4bn buyback at a CNY 600 cap announced on the night of November 23 and orders that already cover 2027. The stock is still 70% below its June peak of CNY 1,415.68 and trades at 19.56 times trailing earnings, with a market cap of CNY 506.7bn. The November 23 spotlight looked at the gap between the company's view and the market's → Zhongji Innolight spotlight.

300502 · Eoptolink

CNY 189.03 (+4.26%). It opened at 185.99, fell to 179.61 and then climbed to 189.70, closing near the high. Turnover of CNY 16.315bn ranked second in the market and its 7.04% turnover ratio was the highest in our group; main funds bought CNY 934m net, including CNY 529m in the closing half hour. There was no filing. Q3 revenue of CNY 10.940bn (+80.30%) and net profit of CNY 3.843bn (+61.15%) missed consensus by a wide margin, but the cause — shipments delayed by flooding in Thailand — was recognised in October, and the company has guided to a sharp Q4 ramp. Of the CNY 800m-1bn buyback approved in October, 4.2163m shares for CNY 800m (average CNY 189.7) had been executed by November 5; today's close is still below that average. Today's spotlight covers the company in depth → Eoptolink spotlight.

688981 · SMIC

CNY 116.50 (-4.51%). Its open of 123.00 was effectively the high at 123.02, and it slid to 115.76 in the afternoon. Turnover was CNY 5.953bn with a 2.53% turnover ratio and CNY 275m of net main-fund selling. SMIC drove the STAR 50's 1.36% decline. There was no company-specific bad news: Q3 revenue of USD 3.19bn (+33.9%), a 29.9% gross margin and 97.3% utilisation beat expectations. The problem is the Q4 guide — revenue +4-6% but gross margin down to 26-28%. 36Kr attributed that to depreciation: unit depreciation per 8-inch equivalent wafer has risen from USD 200 in 2022 to about USD 300 in Q3 2026, or 30% of revenue, which price increases cannot fully cover. TSMC's and Samsung's suspension of sub-16nm work for mainland customers is a near-term revenue drag and a medium-term tailwind for domestic substitution. CICC cut its H-share target to HKD 72 on November 24.

600183 · Shengyi Technology

CNY 61.85 (+5.85%). It opened at 59.50, traded up to 62.80 and held the gain into the close. Turnover of CNY 6.325bn was 1.4 times the previous day's CNY 4.588bn, with a 4.28% turnover ratio and CNY 561m of net main-fund inflow, fourth-largest in electronics. There was no company filing; this was entirely a sector move. The Global Electronics Association's warning that the PCB shortage lasts into the first half of 2027, plus TrendForce's 28.3% AI server shipment growth forecast, lifted the whole chain from glass fibre and resin to finished boards. The numbers stand: Q3 revenue of CNY 14.182bn (+78.8%), net profit of CNY 3.440bn (+238.4%) and a 41.63% gross margin. Market cap is CNY 150.2bn at 19.72 times trailing earnings, still less than half of May's CNY 340bn. The relationship between price and profit was covered on November 24 → Shengyi spotlight.

688072 · Piotech

CNY 456.14 (-2.25%). Its open of 480.00 was the high, and it fell to 443.49 before paring the loss. Turnover was CNY 2.919bn with a 2.25% turnover ratio and CNY 136m of net main-fund outflow. Equipment and materials sold off together: AMEC -6.52%, Hwatsing -5.94%, NAURA -3.14%, with CNY 1.383bn leaving the equipment sector. Nine-month revenue was CNY 5.220bn (+23.70%) and adjusted net profit CNY 928m (+102.66%), with profit still outgrowing revenue. Trailing P/E is 47.58 and market cap CNY 133.3bn.

603986 · GigaDevice

CNY 197.58 (-0.35%). It opened at 202.00, traded up to 202.83, fell to 194.00 and closed at 197.58. Turnover was CNY 4.839bn with a 3.64% turnover ratio and CNY 195m of net main-fund outflow. This was a second down day after Tuesday's 6.69% fall, but the loss was much smaller. Its CNY 1-2bn all-cancellation buyback has passed CNY 1.3bn executed. 688008 · Montage Technology

CNY 124.90 (-1.19%). Its open of 130.00 was the high, and it fell to 123.13. Turnover was CNY 3.107bn with a 2.16% turnover ratio, and main funds were roughly flat with a net CNY 30m inflow. Memory interface chips track server shipments, so they fell with server and storage names. The company's own news remains supportive: nine-month revenue of CNY 5.414bn (+33.4%) and net profit of CNY 3.237bn (+98.3%), with CNY 2.50-2.79bn of interconnect orders booked as of October 26 for fourth-quarter delivery. 688521 · VeriSilicon

CNY 74.84 (-0.94%). It opened at 77.30, traded up to 78.42, fell to 73.28 and closed at 74.84. Its CNY 2.5-5.0bn buyback at a CNY 63.78 cap returns to an extraordinary shareholder meeting on December 9. Trailing P/E is 15.94, market cap CNY 2.2191tn, and the free float remains just 6.6%. What to watch

  1. 01
    Trade

    Washington opens a Section 301 tariff case, China capped at 20%

    On November 25 the US Trade Representative finalised determinations against 14 economies over structural excess capacity and proposed additional tariffs of up to 20% on Chinese goods. That rate assumes the Kuala Lumpur arrangement of last October holds; USTR said China's rate would rise to 40% if the truce collapses. Semiconductors are among the sectors named in the China report. Written comments are due January 5, 2027, hearings begin January 25, and the US-China truce expires January 10. Until the rate is fixed, every negotiating headline lands in exporters' pricing.

    January 5, 10 and 25, 2027
  2. 02
    Policy

    1.6T transport and 6G enter the national network plan

    On November 26 the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Cyberspace Administration and the NDRC published an implementation plan for next-generation communications networks, dated November 17. It calls for better intra-compute, inter-compute and access network performance, deployment of 400G/800G and 1.6T high-speed channels to connect ten-thousand-card intelligent computing clusters, and cross-region compute scheduling, with 6G, ultra-high-speed optical and satellite communications named as technology targets. The same day, eight ministries including Commerce encouraged AI services such as cloud and large models to expand overseas. That is the direct reason optical modules rose in an otherwise weak chip tape.

    November 26 announcement
  3. 03
    Packaging

    Huawei and CXMT take the ISSCC 2027 stage

    The ISSCC 2027 agenda draft published by IEEE on November 23 includes four Huawei papers. One, 'A 64-Channel 4096-DQ Custom HBM with Memory Controllers and 5-Layer Wafer-on-Wafer Hybrid Bonding', combines a memory controller with 4,096 DQ links and five-layer wafer-on-wafer bonding. CXMT will present 32Gb DDR5 and LPDDR6 interface work at the same conference. Acceptance is not volume production, but it is the first public look at how far domestic HBM and next-generation DRAM have travelled. The conference runs February 14-18, 2027 in San Francisco.

    February 14-18, 2027
  4. 04
    Flows

    Flat index, 3,642 decliners

    Turnover fell for a third session to CNY 1.3174tn and breadth widened sharply to 1,766 advancers against 3,642 decliners. Money left semiconductors (CNY 2.567bn net outflow) for components (CNY 4.527bn inflow) and communications equipment (CNY 2.772bn). Banks, coal and oil held the index up; growth stocks have not recovered. With a December Fed hike fully priced before the December 8-9 meeting, turnover and margin balances next week decide the direction.

    December 8-9
  5. 05
    Optics

    Q4 ramp and the first NPO shipments

    The three optical module leaders will refresh Q4 shipment guidance and 2027 volumes in December. Zhongji Innolight says major customer orders cover 2027 and some carry 2028 guidance, while Eoptolink's flood-delayed revenue was recognised in October, making its Q4 ramp steeper. Tencent plans a 3.2T NPO solution in Q4 2026 and Alibaba is testing one. When the 1.6T DSP shortage eases sets the ceiling for next year's volumes.

    December 2026 - 2027
  6. 06
    Memory

    December contract talks test the size of the price gains

    TrendForce expects conventional DRAM contract prices to rise 10-15% in Q4, then 5-10%, 3-8% and 0-5% through 2027. NAND spot prices are down 20% since mid-October, and module makers used up their cheap wafer inventory in Q3, so costs rose first. Q4 margins at CXMT and GigaDevice hinge on December contracts. Results follow in January 2027.

    December contract talks
