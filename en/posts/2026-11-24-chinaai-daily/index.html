China AI Daily Report · November 24

Jensen Huang's reinstatement sent US semis soaring overnight, but A-shares loaded the gain into the opening print and gave it back all day. Shanghai rose 0.27%, ChiNext touched +4% before closing 0.05% lower, and only the STAR 50 held on, up 1.81%. Optical modules and PCB names fell 8–11% from their opening levels, while domestic chips such as Cambricon (+6.03%), SMIC (+4.04%) and Hua Hong (+10.36%) kept their gains to the close.

Including the Beijing exchange the total was CNY 1.668tn (+CNY 103.9bn). Eight stocks traded more than CNY 10bn; four of our eleven names made that list — Zhongji Innolight (CNY 28.583bn), Eoptolink (CNY 24.975bn), Cambricon (CNY 14.321bn) and CXMT (CNY 11.913bn).

The Shanghai Composite closed at 3,548.37 (+0.27%), the Shenzhen Component at 10,423.60 (−0.29%) and ChiNext at 2,278.99 (−0.05%). The opening was a different picture. Shanghai started at 3,567.84, touched 3,555.49 and finished below its opening print; ChiNext gapped up 3.9%, traded more than 4% higher and closed at its low of the day. Only the STAR 50 held its gain, at 1,250.09 (+1.81%). Advancers numbered 1,952 against 3,498 decliners, with the advancer count falling by more than 1,100 in the afternoon. There were 64 limit-ups, 4 limit-downs and a 53.96% failure rate on limit-up boards.

The trigger came overnight. Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after he resigned on November 12, and the stock jumped 31.48% in New York, adding close to USD 1tn of market value. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 14.03%, its biggest one-day gain since 2025. TSMC added 14%, Micron 19%, ASML 9%, and Astera Labs, Credo and SiTime more than 20%. A-shares took the cue and gapped optical modules, fibre and PCB up 8–15%. Then the profit-taking began. AI hardware could not hold its opening levels, and the indices gave back 1–2 percentage points from the highs.

Sectors split. Consumer electronics (smart speakers, wireless earbuds, Apple suppliers, SparkLink, AI phones), shipbuilding, insurance, reducers and semiconductors rose; precious metals, non-ferrous metals, oil, diversified financials and generic drugs fell. Money leaving AI hardware moved into banks, insurers and baijiu. It was a day when individual names mattered more than the index: ChiNext round-tripped a 4% gain into a small loss, while the STAR 50 held +1.81% on Cambricon, SMIC and Hua Hong.

Two themes drove domestic chips. The first is regulation. The BIS interim final rule published on November 16 and effective on November 19 tightened three routes at once: remote access, third-country foundry work and packaging. CSC said the domestic supply chain is now effectively closed-loop, citing Cambricon and MetaX building fully domestic chains, CXMT completing HBM3 adaptation with top cloud customers for 2027 volume, and Tencent's jump in third-quarter capex. The second is share. Bernstein Research expects domestic AI chips, led by Huawei's Ascend, to exceed 50% of the market for the first time in 2026, with domestic output covering demand by 2028 at a 104% supply/demand ratio. That drove the afternoon rally in compute-chip names.

Flows were heavy. Main funds bought CNY 8.57bn of electronics, including CNY 3.047bn of components, and sold machinery, brokers, non-ferrous metals and utilities. Foxconn Industrial Internet drew about CNY 2.2bn, the most in the market, followed by Zhongji Innolight, Cambricon and Dongshan Precision. East Money saw more than CNY 500m leave, with BYD, CXMT and Chaosheng Electronics among the largest sellers. That explains how 3,500 stocks fell on a day the index rose.

688256 · Cambricon

CNY 970.00 (+6.03%) on CNY 14.321bn of turnover and a 2.38% free-float turnover rate. It effectively carried the STAR 50 on its own. The stock gapped up on the Nvidia news, extended to more than 6% in the morning and held near its high even as the index slid in the afternoon. Main funds bought a net CNY 1.082bn (7.55% of turnover), while faster money sold a net CNY 1.076bn. There was no new company filing. The move combined the overnight US rally, Bernstein's 50% share call and expectations that tighter BIS rules push domestic buyers toward local chips. The stock had reclaimed CNY 1,000 on November 12, fallen to 875 after the November 16 export-control revision, and recovered to 970 in two sessions. The structural case and risks are in our November 20 feature → Cambricon spotlight.

688041 · Hygon

CNY 163.01 (−0.60%). It opened at 169.92 (+3.6%), ran to 171.99, then slid to 162.50 and closed negative — a fourth straight decline. Turnover was CNY 5.464bn at a 1.41% turnover rate, among the lightest of our eleven names. The news was supportive: on November 20 the company said its supply chain is stable, CPU and DCU demand keeps expanding, and next-generation development is on schedule. The problem is positioning. Institutional money has been leaving since the October high, so every bounce meets supply. No further execution has been disclosed on the CNY 500–800m buyback since CNY 235m was spent on November 19. See the November 19 feature → Hygon spotlight.

300308 · Zhongji Innolight

CNY 417.00 (−0.05%). It ran to 464.67 (+11.4%) before giving the entire opening gain back. Turnover of CNY 28.583bn was the highest in the A-share market and one of only two above CNY 20bn. The catalyst came late on November 23, when the board approved a CNY 2–4bn buyback with a CNY 600 cap over three months. The prior CNY 5bn programme, completed in September at 5.65m shares and an average of CNY 884, has been switched to full cancellation. Even with a second large buyback in two months, the stock could only hold its opening print. Our November 23 feature covered the gap between the company's price and the market's → Zhongji Innolight spotlight.

300502 · Eoptolink

CNY 180.01 (−0.74%). It reached 198.88 (+9.7%) before returning to the CNY 180 line. Turnover of CNY 24.975bn ranked second, and it also ranked second in after-hours fixed-price trading at CNY 20.27m. There was no new filing; the November 18 statement that customer demand guidance is clear, the order book is full and there is no downgrade or cancellation, plus 1.6T volume production, remains the backdrop. Of the CNY 800m–1bn buyback approved on October 23, CNY 800m had been spent by November 5 on 4.216m shares at an average of CNY 189.7. With the close now below that average, whether the remaining amount is executed becomes the near-term support test.

688981 · SMIC

CNY 126.51 (+4.04%). It opened slightly lower at 121.10, ran to 127.85 and closed near the high. Together with Hua Hong (+10.36%), the foundry pair defended the index. Third-quarter revenue was USD 3.19bn (+6.1% QoQ) at a 29.9% gross margin, both records, and Q4 guidance is +4–6% revenue with a 26–28% margin. Co-CEO Zhao Haijun said 2027 will be a year of severe capacity shortage lasting into mid-2028. The BIS advanced-node rule narrows overseas routes while pushing domestic designers toward domestic foundries; on Tuesday the latter read-through won.

600183 · Shengyi Technology

CNY 61.60 (+1.40%). It opened limit-up at 66.83 and slid to 61.20, giving back 8.4% from the opening print. Turnover was CNY 7.913bn at a 5.09% turnover rate. The gap came from Citi's China industrial survey published on November 23 — the CCL bottleneck sits in AI glass cloth and high-end copper foil, prices keep rising through 2027 and only balance in 2028 — plus Kingboard Laminates' eighth price increase this year and November increases for M6-and-below products. The afternoon slide tracked the broader AI hardware unwind. Q3 revenue was CNY 14.182bn (+78.76%), net profit CNY 3.44bn (+238.40%) and gross margin 41.63%, another quarterly record. We unpack price, earnings and the share price in today's feature → Shengyi Technology spotlight.

688072 · Piotech

CNY 517.34 (+1.78%). It reached 535.30 (+5.3%) before paring the gain, holding up relatively well as semiconductor equipment gapped up and faded. The company-specific news was confirmed on November 20: the CSRC approved its acquisition of 100% of Wuxi Shangji for CNY 2.5bn, adding PVD and dry-etch lines, and chairman Lu Guangquan proposed a second CNY 200–300m buyback. Nine-month revenue was CNY 5.22bn (+23.70%) and non-recurring net profit CNY 928m (+102.66%), with profit growing far faster than sales. Still, third-quarter revenue alone was CNY 2.308bn, up only 1.84% year on year, so the pace at which orders convert into revenue needs watching.

603986 · GigaDevice

CNY 212.50 (−0.98%). It opened at 229.00 (+6.71%), touched 229.50, then fell to 212.03. The early gain came from memory supply reports: most of Samsung's 2027–2029 capacity is contracted, top makers meet only 60–70% of demand, and some customers want deals extended to 2031. Its Hong Kong line (03986) also opened 8.79% higher before fading. Third-quarter results already reflect the price cycle: revenue CNY 8.732bn (+225.65%) and net profit CNY 4.209bn (+728.93%). At the November 17 briefing, management said gradual NOR price increases and SLC NAND shortages would last "for a considerable period", with linear DRAM prices staying high.

688008 · Montage Technology

CNY 133.17 (+0.73%). It opened at 143.00 (+8.06%), reached 143.29 and fell back to 133.00. Its Hong Kong line (06809) jumped 11.61% early before paring most of the gain. The catalyst is deeper AMD cooperation: the two will expand work across memory interface, PCIe interconnect, CXL memory expansion and pooling, and clocking for next-generation AI infrastructure, and Montage's key interconnect chips have passed interoperability validation on AMD's sixth-generation EPYC platform. A 6.76m-share cancellation (0.55% of capital) and a CNY 300–500m second buyback with a CNY 280 cap also eased supply pressure. Q3 revenue was CNY 2.079bn (+46.0%) and net profit CNY 1.240bn (+162.0%), both records; Q4 interconnect order backlog is CNY 2.50–2.79bn.

688521 · VeriSilicon

CNY 83.16 (+4.50%). It traded slightly lower through the morning and jumped in the afternoon as compute-chip names rallied, closing near the day's gain. After a 31% decline last week, this is the second day of recovery. There was no separate filing. The move reflected Bernstein's domestic AI-chip forecast — above 50% share in 2026 and 104% demand coverage in 2028 — plus expectations that BIS rules lift domestic chiplet and IP demand. The medium-term story remains design IP licensing, wearable IP subsystems and chiplet integration; the next check is order momentum disclosed in the third-quarter report and any expansion of ties with domestic foundries and packagers.

688825 · CXMT

CNY 36.26 (+1.00%) on CNY 11.913bn of turnover, fourth among our eleven names. Flows moved separately: main funds sold a net CNY 426m by midday, the third-largest outflow, before turning to a net CNY 59.88m inflow by the close. The CNY 2.5–5bn buyback announced on November 19, of which up to CNY 4bn is for cancellation, is underpinning the price. Third-quarter revenue was CNY 118.714bn (+19.30% QoQ), net profit CNY 54.435bn and gross margin 89.53%, reflecting DRAM prices. Contract liabilities reached CNY 7.036bn at end-September, more than 14 times the start of the year, evidence that long-term supply agreements are accumulating. CSC expects CXMT's HBM3, already adapted with top cloud customers, to enter volume production in 2027.

What to watch

  1. 01
    Rules

    The BIS rule's first week is about licensing speed

    The interim final rule published on November 16 and effective on November 19 tightened remote access, third-country foundry work and packaging at once. A designer must sit on BIS's approved list for a foundry or OSAT to take its advanced-node orders without a licence. Samsung has already told some Chinese customers it will pause deliveries while licences are sought. That is a tailwind for domestic substitution, but the size of the hit only becomes visible once the first approvals and their processing times are known.

    Late November–December
  2. 02
    Flows

    A gap-up that gave back 8–11% in one session

    Zhongji Innolight ran to 464.67 (+11.4%) and closed at 417.00 (−0.05%); Eoptolink went from 198.88 to 180.01. The two names alone traded CNY 53.5bn. The issue is that this money reversed within the day. Whether margin balances in optical modules and PCB hold, and whether turnover stays above CNY 20bn on November 25–27, decides if this was a rebound or a distribution.

    November 25–27
  3. 03
    Earnings

    Q4 guidance and the 2027 price negotiation

    SMIC guided Q4 revenue up 4–6% sequentially with gross margin of 26–28%, below Q3's 29.9%. Hua Hong guided USD 1.08–1.09bn of revenue at 25–26% margin. Both call 2027 the tightest year for capacity. How much of the price increase is locked in during December negotiations sets the starting point for next year's earnings.

    December–January 2027
  4. 04
    Policy

    The 80% margin rule and the first week of leverage

    The Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing exchanges cut the minimum margin ratio for securities lending from 100% to 80%, effective November 30. In theory CNY 1m of collateral now buys CNY 1.25m of stock, roughly CNY 400bn of additional buying power across the market. Monday's rally priced the expectation; the actual test is whether margin balances and new account openings rise in the following week.

    November 30–early December
  5. 05
    Memory

    Samsung's 2027–2029 output is already sold

    Reports said most of Samsung's 2027–2029 memory capacity is locked under contract, top makers are meeting only 60–70% of customer demand, and some customers want long-term deals extended to 2031. The news lifted GigaDevice 6.7% and Montage 8.1% at the open before both faded. Q4 contract prices and allocation volumes are the next checkpoint.

    December contract talks
  6. 06
    Risk

    Reinstated, but the investigation is not closed

    Nvidia's board reinstated Huang while saying the independent investigation continues and a chief compliance officer will be appointed. Both parties in Congress are questioning the process. This rally rests on the removal of a CEO risk, so any headline from the investigation or on export-control compliance can reverse it. Nvidia's Q4 guidance and the size of its China revenue belong in the same check.
