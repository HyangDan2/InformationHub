China AI Spotlight · Shengyi Technology — profit tripled, the share price halved

Q3 revenue of CNY 14.182bn (+78.8%), net profit of CNY 3.44bn (+238.4%) and a 41.63% gross margin — all records. Yet the stock opened limit-up and closed up just 1.40%, and the CNY 340bn market value of May is now CNY 149.6bn. Here is what sits between a material that keeps getting more expensive and the 2027 demand that has to pay for it.

The company and its place in the chain

Shengyi Technology (600183.SH) started in Dongguan, Guangdong, in 1985, listed in Shanghai in 1998, and has ranked second in the world in rigid copper-clad laminate (CCL) sales for 13 consecutive years on Prismark's count. Its 2025 share was 13.2%, after 13.7% in 2024. Kingboard Laminates leads in the mid-14s and Taiwan Union Technology is third at around 13%, with the top three holding 41% of the global market between them.

The company sells two things. The first is CCL and prepreg (PP), which was 64.95% of first-half 2026 revenue. The second is printed circuit boards, run by listed subsidiary Shengyi Electronics (688183.SH), at 29.01%. CCL is the raw material for a PCB, and the PCB goes into AI servers, switches and phones. Because both sit inside the same group, a price increase upstream becomes a cost downstream, and stronger PCB pricing upstream pulls through more laminate volume.

In the value chain Shengyi is the bottom layer of AI hardware. Above it are copper foil (Mitsui, Nanya), electronic glass cloth (Taiwan Glass, Fuqiao, Honghe) and specialty resins; below it are PCB makers and hyperscalers. CCL is only about 1% of a server's bill of materials, so even a doubling of the price lifts the finished cost by roughly 2%. That is why, in a shortage like this one, the increase passes through with comparatively little resistance.

Capacity is being added now. The Jiangxi phase-two project reached full operation in the first half of 2026, adding 18m square metres of CCL and 34m metres of prepreg a year. The Thailand plant starts trial production in the second half of 2026. In April the company committed CNY 5.2bn to a second Songshan Lake plant in Dongguan with 48m sqm (about 38.4m sheets) and 100m metres of prepreg; the first phase is due in 2027. Management puts 2027 capacity at 180m sqm, 30% of it on dedicated M9 lines.

Recent results and the numbers that matter

Third-quarter revenue was CNY 14.182bn (+78.76%) and attributable net profit CNY 3.440bn (+238.40%). Revenue rose 30.30% sequentially and profit 61.61%. Both are quarterly records. Profit excluding non-recurring items was CNY 3.472bn, higher still. Profit growing three times as fast as revenue means price increases and product mix more than covered input-cost inflation.

The margin is the number to watch. Third-quarter gross margin was 41.63%, up more than 9 percentage points from 32.6% in the second quarter and the highest the company has recorded since 2020. For the nine months it was 35.36%, up 8.62pp year on year. Management gives three reasons: high-end products developed earlier entered volume production just as raw material costs spiked, new capacity came on stream in time to dilute fixed costs, and the sales mix shifted upward.

For the nine months, revenue was CNY 33.208bn (+61.10%), net profit CNY 6.727bn (+175.36%) and adjusted profit CNY 6.379bn (+168.10%). Against full-year 2025 revenue of CNY 28.431bn and profit of CNY 3.334bn, nine months of 2026 are already 17% ahead on revenue and 102% ahead on profit. The net margin is 22.5% and return on equity 34.53%. Operating cash flow rose 91% to CNY 6.066bn, so the profit is converting into cash at a reasonable rate.

The balance sheet shows the cost of that growth. Total assets rose 63% from the start of the year to CNY 53.449bn, and the debt ratio climbed 1.98pp to 47.37%. Capital expenditure in the nine months was CNY 3.0bn, up 76% year on year. While the expansion converts into 2027 revenue, borrowing and depreciation rise together.

The two divisions look like this. CCL revenue in the first half was CNY 12.357bn (+47.74%) at a 29.16% gross margin (+5.47pp). Shengyi Electronics reported third-quarter revenue of CNY 3.76bn (+23.0%), net profit of CNY 801m (+37.1%) and a 36.6% gross margin — more profitable than the parent, as AI server boards took a larger share of the mix.

Today's move and the competitive picture

On November 24 Shengyi opened limit-up at CNY 66.83, a full 10% above the previous close of 60.75. It then slid as far as CNY 61.20 — 8.4% below the opening print — and closed at CNY 61.60 (+1.40%), 7.8% under it. Turnover was CNY 7.913bn at a 5.09% turnover rate, the busiest of the past five sessions. The market value is CNY 149.6bn and the trailing price-to-earnings ratio 16.68.

The catalyst arrived late on November 23. Citi published a China industrial survey arguing that the CCL bottleneck sits in AI-grade glass cloth and high-end copper foil, that prices keep rising through 2027 and only balance once new capacity arrives in 2028. Around the same time Kingboard Laminates issued its eighth price increase of the year (October 13, 15% on products using 2116-and-below thin cloth), and Panasonic told customers it would raise some CCL prices by 200% from 2027. The Global Electronics Association warned on November 20 that the constraint is not finished PCB capacity but glass fibre, resin, copper foil and equipment, lasting at least through the first half of 2027.

The raw-material numbers are more dramatic than the laminate prices. According to Citi, 1080-grade electronic glass cloth went from CNY 5.0/m in December 2025 to CNY 20.8/m in November 2026, 2116 from 4.9 to 20.2, and mainstream 7628 from 4.6 to 14.6. Loom supply is the bottleneck, so thin cloth rose far more than thick. CCL makers passed the cost down and, in a shortage, widened their margins at the same time.

Competition splits three ways. Kingboard is vertically integrated across glass fibre, copper foil and resin, which gives it pricing power in the low and mid range and the number-one share in the mid-14s. Taiwan Union Technology leads in the highest grades such as M8, reportedly selling at USD 100 a sheet. Nanya New Material lifted its AI CCL mix from 8–9% to nearly 30% and its gross margin from 11.8% in 2025 to 38.1% in the third quarter of 2026. Shengyi sits between them, taking only selected M4 orders and concentrating resources on M6 and above. Margins are better there, but pricing power in that segment still rests with the international suppliers such as TUC and Panasonic.

That is also why the market values the company cheaply. In May 2026 its market value was CNY 340bn; today it is CNY 149.6bn. Profit has tripled while the share price has halved. The shortage running through 2027 helps earnings, but the capacity arriving in 2028 may confirm that today's margin was the peak. The stock is also sensitive to qualification rumours: on November 12, talk that Nvidia had cancelled M9 orders over quality knocked sentiment, and the company said it had received no cancellation notice and that its order book was full.

What to watch and what could go wrong

  1. 01
    Pricing

    How much of the November increase lands in Q4

    The company raised prices on M6-and-below laminate and prepreg from November, and orders reportedly stretch to January 2027 — though the company has not confirmed that figure. Q3's 41.63% margin was earned on raw material bought before the latest leg up, plus a richer product mix. In Q4, glass cloth and copper foil cost more, so the degree to which price increases were passed through becomes visible in the numbers.

    Q4 and FY results, February–March 2027
  2. 02
    Margins

    What has to hold for a 41.6% gross margin

    The gain came from a higher share of M6-and-above product and from the Jiangxi phase-two line running at full rate, which diluted fixed costs. If 2027 price negotiations bring larger cuts, or glass-cloth supply gaps cut line utilisation, the margin falls immediately. Management itself concedes that pricing power in high-end product sits with the international suppliers, so the ceiling is set by mix rather than by raw materials.

    Every quarter
  3. 03
    Supply

    The 2028 capacity wave

    Nanya New Material is aiming for 6.4m sheets a month by Q1 2028, Jin'an Guoju starts trial output in 2027, and Shengyi is preparing its Songshan Lake plant (38.4m sheets a year) and Thailand. Citi sees tight supply through 2027 and balance only when new capacity arrives in 2028. If today's margin is the cycle peak, that is precisely why the market discounts the stock first.

    2027–2028
  4. 04
    Orders

    M9 and M8 adoption, Thailand and Songshan Lake ramp

    On November 12 a rumour said Nvidia had cancelled M9 orders over quality; the company said it had received no cancellation notice and that its order book is full. The real confirmation comes through customer qualification and shipments. Nvidia's Vera Rubin generation and its use of M8 and M9, plus yields at the Thailand plant (trial production in H2 2026) and the Songshan Lake phase one (2027), set the top end of next year's revenue.

    Q4 2026–2027
  5. 05
    Risk

    Customer concentration and export exposure

    Overseas sales were 24.4% of 2025 revenue (CNY 6.808bn), including US PCB customers and European and Asian distribution. Tighter BIS rules on remote access and contract manufacturing can shift the regional mix of end demand, and currency moves sit between input costs and selling prices. The offset is that the same rules push Chinese AI server build-outs toward domestic suppliers.
