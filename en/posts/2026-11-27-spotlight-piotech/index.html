China AI Spotlight · Piotech — how the thin-film leader earns its keep, with domestic tools past 50%

Third-quarter revenue of CNY 2.308bn was a quarterly record and non-GAAP net profit of CNY 561m more than doubled from the second quarter. Gross margin reached 42.47%, up from 35.0% for all of 2025. The headline CNY 2.431bn of nine-month net profit is mostly a CNY 1.616bn fair-value gain on listed stakes; the operating business doubled on a non-GAAP basis. Today's limit-up was the sector, not the company: reports that domestic tools took more than half of purchases on the third-phase line of a leading memory maker, SEMI's USD 149bn 2026 front-end forecast and China's first equipment quality standard all landed on the same day. The problem is the price: 57 times trailing earnings and about 2% below the highest analyst target.

Company and position in the value chain

Piotech (688072.SH) is a front-end semiconductor equipment maker based in Shenyang, listed on the STAR Market in April 2022. Its core business is thin-film deposition, one of the three pillars of front-end processing alongside lithography and etch. Within deposition it focuses on the chemical-vapour family, above all PECVD (plasma-enhanced chemical vapour deposition), and has added ALD, gap-fill tools (SACVD, HDPCVD, Flowable CVD) and hybrid-bonding equipment for 3D stacking.

The revenue mix is narrow. Of CNY 6.519bn of consolidated revenue in 2025, PECVD contributed CNY 5.142bn, or 78.9%. ALD added CNY 301m (4.6%), hybrid bonding CNY 136m (2.1%), and the remaining CNY 940m came from gap-fill tools, parts and services. The company was built on one product line, and the fact that this line is effectively the only domestic volume supplier is both its value and its vulnerability. Piotech says the combination of PECVD, ALD, SACVD, HDPCVD and Flowable CVD covers about 100 process applications needed for advanced memory and logic. By the end of October 2026 it had shipped more than 4,400 reactor chambers into about 100 production lines, with tool uptime above 90%, which it says matches international peers.

Upstream sit vacuum pumps, RF power supplies, mass-flow controllers and electrostatic chucks, some of which are still imported; Piotech multi-sources key parts and co-develops them with strategic suppliers. Downstream are the memory and logic fabs. The company does not name customers, but the market treats CXMT, YMTC, SMIC and Hua Hong as the core customer base. In practice Piotech's revenue tracks the capital spending of China's memory and logic capacity.

The market arithmetic, using SEMI figures the company cites: global chip-making equipment sales were USD 115.7bn in 2025 (+11%), of which deposition is about 22%, or USD 25.5bn. PECVD is 33% of deposition, ALD 11% and other CVD 6%. Mainland China is 36.51% of global equipment sales, implying a Chinese deposition market of about USD 9.3bn. But Applied Materials, Lam Research and Tokyo Electron hold about 70% of the global CVD market, according to Gartner. Piotech's share is still small. Outside analysis puts its domestic PECVD share near 70% but its share of deposition overall at about 5%. That is where the phrase "deep but narrow moat" comes from.

Widening it is the current strategy. First, PVD: the Wuxi Shangji acquisition, registered in November 2026, adds PVD and etch so the company can sell CVD, ALD and PVD as one package. Second, 3D integration: after the Dione 300 wafer-to-wafer hybrid bonder went into production in 2023, the faster, more precise Dione 300 eX passed customer validation in the third quarter of 2026. Piotech says it matches the most advanced international tools on alignment and bonding precision and throughput, and it is the only domestic company to have commercialised hybrid bonding. Besi holds about 67% of the global market and estimates demand of 1,400 sets a year by 2030, a USD 2.8bn market at USD 2m a tool.

Recent results and key metrics

Third-quarter revenue was CNY 2.308bn (+1.84% YoY, +28.17% QoQ), a quarterly record. The flat year-on-year comparison reflects a high prior-year base and the mix of tools accepted in the period; revenue recognition depends on which platforms and processes clear customer acceptance. The quarterly path tells the story better: CNY 1.113bn in the first quarter, CNY 1.800bn in the second and CNY 2.308bn in the third.

Profit grew faster. Third-quarter net profit attributable to shareholders was CNY 1.088bn (+135.39%) and non-GAAP net profit CNY 561m (+33.63% YoY, +111.61% QoQ). For the nine months, revenue was CNY 5.220bn (+23.70%), net profit CNY 2.431bn (+336.79%), non-GAAP net profit CNY 929m (+102.66%), basic EPS CNY 8.55 and weighted average ROE 25.75%.

Margin is the heart of the story. Gross margin fell from 41.7% in 2024 to 35.0% in 2025 as new products carried early-stage yields and validation costs. It recovered to 41.65% in the first nine months of 2026 (+8.37pp year on year) and reached 42.47% in the third quarter (+8.05pp YoY, +1.89pp QoQ). The company credits production scale as advanced products enter volume, which lowers unit cost. The 4,400 chambers and 100 lines are the evidence of that scale.

There is, however, an optical illusion in the headline profit. Fair-value gains rose CNY 1.616bn year on year in the first nine months, including CNY 684m in the third quarter, on listed stakes in Hengyunchang, Kema Technology and Xianfeng Jingke. No cash arrives, and a falling share price reverses the gain. Strip it out and the operating business earned CNY 929m. Share-based payments are still inside that number.

Orders look solid. Backlog was about CNY 11.0bn at the end of 2025, and contract liabilities reached a record CNY 5.131bn at the end of June 2026, from CNY 4.852bn at end-2025. Shipped goods were CNY 4.512bn, up 2.18% from year-end. The company says new orders are growing quickly, several new customers have been added and overseas orders have been won and exported. Against that, inventory of CNY 8.833bn is 34.9% of total assets and inventory days reached 764 in the third quarter. Operating cash flow, positive at CNY 3.633bn in 2025, turned to negative CNY 36m in the first nine months of 2026.

Today's issue and the competitive picture

On November 27 Piotech opened at CNY 457.70, dipped to CNY 450.83 and closed at the CNY 547.37 limit (+20.00%), having opened the board once and sealed CNY 56.8m. Turnover was CNY 4.405bn with 3.09% turnover and CNY 359m of net main-fund inflow, 8.23% of the day's value. The limit-up was attributed to "semiconductor equipment + M&A + thin-film deposition".

The trigger was the sector, not the company. Morning reports said new orders at AMEC, ACM Research and other domestic equipment makers had hit records in the first nine months, and that domestic tools took more than half of purchases on the third-phase line of a leading memory maker for the first time. Jingce Electronics had disclosed a CNY 199.8m front-end metrology contract after the close on November 26, and ACM's backlog of CNY 17.073bn (+88.20%) was recalled. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology approved the industry's first dedicated quality-management standard for semiconductor equipment and components. SEMI expects global 300mm front-end equipment spending of USD 149bn in 2026, up 31%. National Bureau of Statistics data showed electronics profits up 1.0x in January-October, contributing 9.7 percentage points to industrial profit growth. Orders, standards and profits arrived on the same day.

Flows were strong across the sector. Semiconductors rose 14.97% on CNY 280.4bn of turnover, the "Big Fund" theme added 14.77%, the "SMIC concept" 14.47% and "semiconductor equipment" 13.98%. Naura hit its cap at CNY 527.38, and AMEC, ACM, Changchuan Technology, Skyverse and Jingce all rose by 20% or hit their caps. Piotech represents the deposition axis of that move. The quality of the rally, though, was not heavy: the board opened once and the seal was only CNY 56.8m, which says the 20% was sector beta rather than a stock-specific re-rating.

The competitive picture has two layers. Globally, Applied Materials, Lam Research and Tokyo Electron hold 70% of CVD. Domestically, outside analysis puts localisation at only 20-25%, and Piotech dominates PECVD within it. The catch is that this dominance holds only inside PECVD. Fabs buy deposition as a bundle: PECVD for the bulk dielectric, HDPCVD and SACVD to fill narrow gaps, ALD for thin conformal films inside deep holes and PVD for metal lines. Without a platform, pricing power slips.

That is why Wuxi Shangji matters. Shangji makes PVD tools that lead the domestic market in power semiconductors, MEMS and RF, plus dry-etch tools for MEMS. After the deal Piotech will have deposition (CVD, ALD, PVD), etch, bonding and metrology for a "3D integration" line-up. The consideration for 82.97% of Shangji plus the two holding vehicles is CNY 2.5bn, a 289.84% premium to book. Revenue commitments are CNY 350m, 450m and 610m for 2026-28, and the compensation cap covers 68.58% of the consideration. The Shanghai Stock Exchange review cleared on November 13 and CSRC registration on November 20; share issuance, asset transfer and a CNY 1.82bn placement remain.

Competitors are not standing still. Naura already sells PVD, CVD, etch and cleaning as a platform and is several times Piotech's size on 2025 revenue. AMEC is extending customer relationships built on etch into deposition, and ACM Research has added PECVD. Piotech's edge is PECVD process depth and hybrid bonding. When the fourth-phase lines of China's three memory makers start ordering in 2027, bundled purchasing becomes the norm and platform completeness will decide both price and share.

What to watch and the risks

  1. 01
    Orders

    How fast the CNY 5.131bn of contract liabilities turns into revenue

    Contract liabilities reached a record CNY 5.131bn at the end of June, against a backlog of about CNY 11.0bn at end-2025. Tools are recognised only after installation, commissioning and multi-stage process validation, so orders and revenue are separated by months. The flat year-on-year comparison in the third quarter came from the mix of tools accepted. The annual report in January 2027 will show how much of the contract liability and the CNY 4.512bn of shipped goods at June converted into revenue in the fourth quarter.

    January 2027 annual report
  2. 02
    M&A

    The CNY 2.5bn Wuxi Shangji deal adds PVD to the platform

    Piotech is buying 82.97% of Wuxi Shangji plus two holding vehicles that own the rest for a total of CNY 2.5bn. Shangji makes PVD tools that lead the domestic market in power semiconductors, MEMS and RF, and dry-etch tools for MEMS. The valuation is 289.84% above book, and the revenue commitments are CNY 350m for 2026, CNY 450m for 2027 and CNY 610m for 2028. The compensation cap covers only 68.58% of the consideration, which is worth noting. The Shanghai Stock Exchange review cleared on November 13 and CSRC registration on November 20; share issuance, asset transfer and a CNY 1.82bn supporting placement remain.

  4. 04
    Cash flow

    CNY 8.8bn of inventory, 764 days of turnover

    Inventory stood at CNY 8.833bn at the end of June, 34.9% of total assets. Inventory days reached 764 in the third quarter, roughly twice the level at Naura and AMEC. Building tools ahead of acceptance is part of the business, but a slowdown in orders would turn that inventory into write-downs. Operating cash flow was negative CNY 36m in the first nine months, against positive CNY 2.565bn a year earlier. Receivable days, at 65.6, are the shortest among peers.

    January 2027
  5. 05
    Valuation

    Target CNY 560.55, about 2.4% above today's close

    Guotai Haitong on November 22 forecast 2026 net profit of CNY 2.730bn, EPS of CNY 9.34 and a target price of CNY 560.55. Today's close of CNY 547.37 is 97.6% of that target. On Changjiang Securities' 2026 estimate of CNY 3.167bn (November 12) the stock trades at 50.5 times, and on Guotai Haitong's at 58.6 times. Even on 2027 estimates of CNY 3.3-4.6bn it is 35-48 times. The growth is real; the margin of safety after a 20% day is not double-digit.

    Ongoing
  6. 06
    Competition

    70% of PECVD, 5% of deposition overall

    Applied Materials, Lam Research and Tokyo Electron hold about 70% of the global CVD market, according to Gartner. Piotech is effectively the only domestic volume supplier in the PECVD segment, where outside analysis puts its domestic share near 70%, but only about 5% of the total deposition market. Naura sells PVD and CVD as a platform, AMEC is extending from etch into deposition and ACM Research has added PECVD. From 2027 the fight over price and share gets serious.
