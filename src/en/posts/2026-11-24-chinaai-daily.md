## Market session

The Shanghai Composite closed at <strong>3,548.37 (+0.27%)</strong>, the Shenzhen Component at <strong>10,423.60 (−0.29%)</strong> and ChiNext at <strong>2,278.99 (−0.05%)</strong>. The opening was a different picture. Shanghai started at 3,567.84, touched 3,555.49 and finished below its opening print; ChiNext gapped up 3.9%, traded more than 4% higher and closed at its <strong>low of the day</strong>. Only the STAR 50 held its gain, at <strong>1,250.09 (+1.81%)</strong>. Advancers numbered 1,952 against 3,498 decliners, with the advancer count falling by more than 1,100 in the afternoon. There were 64 limit-ups, 4 limit-downs and a 53.96% failure rate on limit-up boards.

The trigger came overnight. Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO 11 days after he resigned on November 12, and the stock jumped <strong>31.48%</strong> in New York, adding close to USD 1tn of market value. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose <strong>14.03%</strong>, its biggest one-day gain since 2025. TSMC added 14%, Micron 19%, ASML 9%, and Astera Labs, Credo and SiTime more than 20%. A-shares took the cue and gapped optical modules, fibre and PCB up 8–15%. Then the profit-taking began. AI hardware could not hold its opening levels, and the indices gave back 1–2 percentage points from the highs.

Sectors split. Consumer electronics (smart speakers, wireless earbuds, Apple suppliers, SparkLink, AI phones), shipbuilding, insurance, reducers and semiconductors rose; precious metals, non-ferrous metals, oil, diversified financials and generic drugs fell. Money leaving AI hardware moved into banks, insurers and baijiu. It was a day when individual names mattered more than the index: ChiNext round-tripped a 4% gain into a small loss, while the STAR 50 held <strong>+1.81%</strong> on Cambricon, SMIC and Hua Hong.

Two themes drove domestic chips. The first is regulation. The BIS interim final rule published on November 16 and effective on November 19 tightened three routes at once: remote access, third-country foundry work and packaging. CSC said the domestic supply chain is now effectively closed-loop, citing Cambricon and MetaX building fully domestic chains, CXMT completing HBM3 adaptation with top cloud customers for 2027 volume, and Tencent's jump in third-quarter capex. The second is share. Bernstein Research expects domestic AI chips, led by Huawei's Ascend, to exceed 50% of the market for the first time in 2026, with domestic output covering demand by 2028 at a 104% supply/demand ratio. That drove the afternoon rally in compute-chip names.

Flows were heavy. Main funds bought <strong>CNY 8.57bn</strong> of electronics, including CNY 3.047bn of components, and sold machinery, brokers, non-ferrous metals and utilities. Foxconn Industrial Internet drew about CNY 2.2bn, the most in the market, followed by Zhongji Innolight, Cambricon and Dongshan Precision. East Money saw more than CNY 500m leave, with BYD, CXMT and Chaosheng Electronics among the largest sellers. That explains how 3,500 stocks fell on a day the index rose.

## China AI watchlist

### 688256 · Cambricon

<strong>CNY 970.00 (+6.03%)</strong> on <strong>CNY 14.321bn</strong> of turnover and a 2.38% free-float turnover rate. It was one of the main supports for the STAR 50. The stock gapped up on the Nvidia news, extended to more than 6% in the morning and held near its high even as the index slid in the afternoon. Main funds bought a net CNY 1.082bn (7.55% of turnover), while faster money sold a net CNY 1.076bn. There was no new company filing. The move combined the overnight US rally, Bernstein's 50% share call and expectations that tighter BIS rules push domestic buyers toward local chips. The stock had reclaimed CNY 1,000 on November 12, sank to an intraday 875.90 on November 23 after the November 16 export-control revision, and returned to 970 on Tuesday. The structural case and risks are in our November 20 feature → [Cambricon spotlight]({{ '/en/posts/2026-11-20-spotlight-cambricon/' | url }}).

### 688041 · Hygon

<strong>CNY 163.01 (−0.60%)</strong>. It opened at 169.92 (+3.6%), ran to <strong>171.99</strong>, then slid to 162.50 and closed negative — a fourth straight decline. Turnover was CNY 5.464bn at a 1.41% turnover rate, among the lightest of our eleven names. The news was supportive: on November 20 the company said its supply chain is stable, CPU and DCU demand keeps expanding, and next-generation development is on schedule. The problem is positioning. Trading has thinned since the October high, so every bounce meets supply. No further execution has been disclosed on the CNY 500–800m buyback since CNY 235m was spent on November 19. See the November 19 feature → [Hygon spotlight]({{ '/en/posts/2026-11-19-spotlight-hygon/' | url }}).

### 300308 · Zhongji Innolight

<strong>CNY 417.00 (−0.05%)</strong>. It ran to <strong>464.67 (+11.4%)</strong> before giving the entire opening gain back. Turnover of <strong>CNY 28.583bn</strong> was the highest in the A-share market and one of only two above CNY 20bn. The catalyst came late on November 23, when the board approved a <strong>CNY 2–4bn buyback</strong> with a CNY 600 cap over three months. The prior CNY 5bn programme, completed in September at 5.65m shares and an average of CNY 884, has been switched to full cancellation. Even with a second large buyback in two months, the stock could only hold its opening print. Our November 23 feature covered the gap between the company's price and the market's → [Zhongji Innolight spotlight]({{ '/en/posts/2026-11-23-spotlight-innolight/' | url }}).

### 300502 · Eoptolink

<strong>CNY 180.01 (−0.74%)</strong>. It reached 198.88 (+9.7%) before returning to the CNY 180 line. Turnover of <strong>CNY 24.975bn</strong> ranked second, and it also ranked second in after-hours fixed-price trading at CNY 20.27m. There was no new filing; the November 18 statement that customer demand guidance is clear, the order book is full and there is no downgrade or cancellation, plus 1.6T volume production, remains the backdrop. Of the CNY 800m–1bn buyback approved on October 23, CNY 800m had been spent by November 5 on 4.216m shares at an average of CNY 189.7. With the close now below that average, whether the remaining amount is executed becomes the near-term support test.

### 688981 · SMIC

<strong>CNY 126.51 (+4.04%)</strong>. It opened slightly lower at 121.10, ran to <strong>127.85</strong> and closed near the high. Together with Hua Hong (+10.36%), the foundry pair defended the index. Third-quarter revenue was USD 3.19bn (+6.1% QoQ) at a 29.9% gross margin, both records, and Q4 guidance is +4–6% revenue with a 26–28% margin. Co-CEO Zhao Haijun said 2027 will be a year of severe capacity shortage lasting into mid-2028. The BIS advanced-node rule narrows overseas routes while pushing domestic designers toward domestic foundries; on Tuesday the latter read-through won.

### 600183 · Shengyi Technology

<strong>CNY 61.60 (+1.40%)</strong>. It opened <strong>limit-up at 66.83</strong> and slid as far as <strong>61.20</strong>, 8.4% below that opening print, before closing 7.8% lower at 61.60. Turnover was CNY 7.913bn at a 5.09% turnover rate. The gap came from Citi's China industrial survey published on November 23 — the CCL bottleneck sits in AI glass cloth and high-end copper foil, prices keep rising through 2027 and only balance in 2028 — plus Kingboard Laminates' eighth price increase this year and November increases for M6-and-below products. The afternoon slide tracked the broader AI hardware unwind. Q3 revenue was CNY 14.182bn (+78.76%), net profit CNY 3.44bn (+238.40%) and gross margin 41.63%, another quarterly record. We unpack price, earnings and the share price in today's feature → [Shengyi Technology spotlight]({{ '/en/posts/2026-11-24-spotlight-shengyi/' | url }}).

### 688072 · Piotech

<strong>CNY 517.34 (+1.78%)</strong>. It reached <strong>535.30 (+5.3%)</strong> before paring the gain, holding up relatively well as semiconductor equipment gapped up and faded. The company-specific news was confirmed on November 20: the CSRC approved its acquisition of 100% of Wuxi Shangji for CNY 2.5bn, adding PVD and dry-etch lines, and chairman Lu Guangquan proposed a second CNY 200–300m buyback. Nine-month revenue was CNY 5.22bn (+23.70%) and non-recurring net profit CNY 928m (+102.66%), with profit growing far faster than sales. Still, third-quarter revenue alone was CNY 2.308bn, up only 1.84% year on year, so the pace at which orders convert into revenue needs watching.

### 603986 · GigaDevice

<strong>CNY 212.50 (−0.98%)</strong>. It opened at 229.00 (+6.71%), touched 229.50, then fell to <strong>212.03</strong>. The early gain came from memory supply reports: most of Samsung's 2027–2029 capacity is contracted, top makers meet only 60–70% of demand, and some customers want deals extended to 2031. Its Hong Kong line (03986) also opened 8.79% higher before fading. Third-quarter results already reflect the price cycle: revenue CNY 8.732bn (+225.65%) and net profit CNY 4.209bn (+728.93%). At the November 17 briefing, management said gradual NOR price increases and SLC NAND shortages would last "for a considerable period", with linear DRAM prices staying high.

### 688008 · Montage Technology

<strong>CNY 133.17 (+0.73%)</strong>. It opened at 143.00 (+8.06%), reached 143.29 and fell back to <strong>133.00</strong>. Its Hong Kong line (06809) jumped 11.61% early before paring most of the gain. The catalyst is deeper AMD cooperation: the two will expand work across memory interface, PCIe interconnect, CXL memory expansion and pooling, and clocking for next-generation AI infrastructure, and Montage's key interconnect chips have passed interoperability validation on AMD's sixth-generation EPYC platform. A proposed 6.76m-share cancellation (0.55% of capital, still subject to shareholder approval) and a CNY 300–500m second buyback with a CNY 280 cap also eased supply pressure. Q3 revenue was CNY 2.079bn (+46.0%) and net profit CNY 1.240bn (+162.0%), both records; Q4 interconnect order backlog is CNY 2.50–2.79bn.

### 688521 · VeriSilicon

<strong>CNY 83.16 (+4.50%)</strong>. It traded slightly lower through the morning and jumped in the afternoon as compute-chip names rallied, closing near the day's gain. After a 31% decline last week, this is the second day of recovery. There was no separate filing. The move reflected Bernstein's domestic AI-chip forecast — above 50% share in 2026 and 104% demand coverage in 2028 — plus expectations that BIS rules lift domestic chiplet and IP demand. The medium-term story remains design IP licensing, wearable IP subsystems and chiplet integration; the next check is order momentum disclosed in the third-quarter report and any expansion of ties with domestic foundries and packagers.

### 688825 · CXMT

<strong>CNY 36.26 (+1.00%)</strong> on <strong>CNY 11.913bn</strong> of turnover, fourth among our eleven names. Flows moved separately: main funds sold a net CNY 426m by midday, the third-largest outflow, before turning to a net CNY 59.88m inflow by the close. The <strong>CNY 2.5–5bn buyback</strong> announced on November 19, of which up to CNY 4bn is for cancellation, is underpinning the price. Third-quarter revenue was CNY 118.714bn (+19.30% QoQ), net profit CNY 54.435bn and gross margin 89.53%, reflecting DRAM prices. Contract liabilities reached CNY 7.036bn at end-September, more than 14 times the start of the year, evidence that long-term supply agreements are accumulating. CSC expects CXMT's HBM3, already adapted with top cloud customers, to enter volume production in 2027.
