## Market

The Shanghai Composite closed at <strong>3,476.47 (-2.03%)</strong>, losing 3,500. The Shenzhen Component fell to <strong>10,086.36 (-3.24%)</strong>, ChiNext to <strong>2,183.58 (-4.19%)</strong>, the STAR 50 to <strong>1,186.14 (-5.12%)</strong>, the STAR Composite to <strong>1,317.53 (-5.93%)</strong> and the BSE 50 to <strong>755.87 (-4.75%)</strong>. ChiNext is at its lowest since July 2025 and has halved from its June 25 peak. There were 586 advancers against 4,926 decliners, 51 limit-ups and more than 280 stocks down 9% or more. Turnover was CNY 1.3831tn, down CNY 272bn on the day. The volume did not vanish; most of it was selling.

The trigger came from the US. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index dropped <strong>7.88%</strong>, with all thirty members lower. Nvidia gave back half of Monday's 31% gain, falling 5.86%, while Micron lost 10.97%, AMD 8.9% and TSMC 4%. Anthropic closing below its IPO price (-6.54%) and a spike in CoreWeave bond yields revived the argument about returns on AI capital spending. Asia followed: the KOSPI fell 6.16% and halted trading, with Samsung Electronics down 9.87%, and the Nikkei was off as much as 3.6% intraday. A December Fed hike priced at 98%, a 10-year Treasury near 4.9% and reports of an imminent tariff announcement covering dozens of economies added to the pressure.

There was a domestic layer too. On November 23 the US Bureau of Industry and Security added seven entities to its export-control list and restricted advanced AI chip access for data centres providing compute to Chinese customers. That helps import substitution in the medium term, but on the day the tightening itself pushed investors to reduce risk. Semiconductors fell 8.30%, lithography 7.78%, the HBM complex 8.08%, and equipment and materials names went with them: Hua Hong -13.89%, NAURA -9.55%, AMEC -7.46%, Hangzhou Changchuan -9.88%. Money went the other way, into influenza and artemisinin drugs, retail, banks, coal and baijiu. That is how 4,900 stocks fell while the index lost 2%.

The exception was optical modules. MOFCOM said it would suspend the second clause of its 2024 Notice No. 46 on dual-use exports to the US from November 28 through January 27, 2027. Read as a signal of easing, it drew buyers into the export chain tied to North American cloud customers, while the same reading weakened the case for urgent domestic substitution and added to selling in local chip names. TFC Optical rose <strong>7.65%</strong> and was the market's largest main-fund inflow at CNY 1.086bn. Zhongji Innolight ran to CNY 429.42 (+3.0%) intraday before closing at CNY 409.00 (-1.92%), still with the largest turnover in the market. Eoptolink opened at CNY 175.10, touched CNY 188.56 and closed at <strong>CNY 181.30 (+0.72%)</strong> — the only gainer among the eleven.

Flows moved to defensives. Main funds sold CNY 48.968bn across the market, with CNY 14.499bn leaving the electronics sector alone, while retail, food and beverage and chemical pharma took money in. By stock, CXMT saw CNY 1.791bn of net main-fund selling, the most in the market, and GigaDevice was second at CNY 906m. On the buy side, TFC Optical was followed by Qingshan Paper, BOE and Kweichow Moutai. Policy is pulling the other way: the three stabilisation measures announced on November 22 — an 80% minimum margin ratio from November 30, lower trading fees and a planned market-maker system for ChiNext — were followed on November 25 by CNY 800bn of MLF. But margin balances have fallen from about CNY 3tn in June to below CNY 2.2tn on November 20, so the capacity policy creates will take time to turn into buying.

### 300502 · Eoptolink

<strong>CNY 181.30 (+0.72%)</strong>. It opened at 175.10 (-2.7%), reached <strong>188.56 (+4.7%)</strong> and held a gain into the close — the only one of the eleven. Of the CNY 800m–1bn buyback approved in October, CNY 800m (4.216m shares at an average of CNY 189.7) had been executed by November 5. The close is still below that average, so whether the remaining programme supports the floor is the near-term question.

### 688008 · Montage Technology
