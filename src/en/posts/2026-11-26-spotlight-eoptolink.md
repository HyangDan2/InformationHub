## Company and position in the value chain

Eoptolink (`300502.SZ`) listed on ChiNext in 2016 and does one thing: optical modules. Optical interconnect products were 99.87% of revenue in the first half of 2026, and most of that is high-speed modules for data centres and AI clusters. Of CNY 24.842bn of 2025 revenue, CNY 24.771bn came from optical interconnect products. The business is simple, which means the cycle is the earnings.

The line-up runs from 400G and 800G pluggables to 1.6T, plus LPO, LRO, XPO, NPO and CPO form factors. It was the first company to mass-produce linear-drive pluggable (LPO) optics, and in March 2026 it launched a 1.6T DR4 module using 400G-per-lane IMDD, a 6.4T NPO solution and the industry's first 12.8T XPO pluggable. It also has optical circuit switches (NX200/300) built on its own 3D MEMS. The technology platforms are VCSEL, EML, silicon photonics and thin-film lithium niobate.

In the chain, this is mostly the assembly end. Upstream sit optical chips (EML and CW lasers) and electrical chips (DSPs), drivers and TIAs, while optical engines, FAUs and connectors come from component makers such as TFC Optical. DSPs are effectively split between Broadcom and Marvell, so the 1.6T bottleneck sits with 3nm foundry capacity and chip designers, not here. Downstream are switch and server vendors and, ultimately, North American hyperscalers and AI infrastructure companies; the market understands Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, Google and the Nvidia ecosystem to be customers.

Manufacturing is in Chengdu and Thailand. The Thai subsidiary (THB 4.661bn of registered capital) generated CNY 21.909bn of revenue and CNY 1.409bn of net profit in 2025, 88.2% of consolidated revenue. Moving production to Thailand is the industry's standard answer to tariffs and rules of origin, but it also means large exposure to Thailand. First-half capacity of 28.36m units is 1.9x the 15.20m of a year earlier, much of the increase from the second Thai phase.

By size it is third in the world. LightCounting's 2024 ranking put it third, up from seventh in 2023; Zhongji Innolight is first and Huawei fourth. Domestic sales were just 3.84% of revenue in 2025, with 96.16% overseas. Today's close of CNY 189.03 gives a market cap of CNY 263.6bn, about half of Zhongji Innolight's CNY 506.7bn.

## Recent results and key metrics

Q3 revenue was <strong>CNY 10.940bn</strong> (+80.30%) and net profit attributable to shareholders <strong>CNY 3.843bn</strong> (+61.15%). Sequentially that is -12.98% revenue and -19.08% profit, the first decline in two quarters. Against Citi and Bloomberg consensus the miss was large: revenue 47.6% below and net profit 54.4% below.

The cause was logistics, not demand. Late September is normally when this company concentrates shipments, and flooding in Thailand blocked airport transport and export clearance. Customers recognise revenue differently — on shipment, on customs clearance, or when they draw from a VMI warehouse — so product that had been built or shipped did not become September revenue. The company said nothing was damaged and no impairment was taken, and that much of the delayed volume was recognised in October.

For the nine months, revenue was <strong>CNY 31.850bn</strong> (+92.97%) and net profit <strong>CNY 11.372bn</strong> (+79.74%), with basic EPS of CNY 8.16 and a weighted ROE of 49.22%. Non-recurring-adjusted profit of CNY 11.140bn is almost identical, so the earnings quality is clean. Operating cash flow, however, fell 68.42% to CNY 1.464bn as receivables rose 68.72% to CNY 7.487bn, inventory 140.70% to CNY 17.413bn and prepayments from CNY 17m to CNY 1.322bn.

Margins have turned. Gross margin was 47.81% for 2025, just over 49% in Q1 2026, 47.96% in Q2 and <strong>46.3%</strong> in Q3. Early 1.6T volumes carry lower yields, the company prioritised volume with large customers, and mix shifted. Management called the Q3 move normal, but it has now come down two quarters running from 48.91% in Q4 2025.

Capacity tells the more useful story. First-half capacity was 28.36m units, output 10.91m and sales 11.19m — only about 38% of capacity ran. DSP and EML supply could not keep up with the equipment build-out, and the company acknowledged in Q3 that insufficient material preparation hurt output and delivery. Put the other way, if materials ease in Q4 there is that much room to produce. That is the basis for management's confidence that Q4 capacity and output will rise sharply.

Prices are still holding. On November 18 the company said customer demand guidance is clear, the backlog is sufficient and no orders have been cancelled, and that 800G and 1.6T prices did not fall in Q3. On next year's pricing pressure it said it sees none "at present".

## Today's issue and the competitive picture

On November 26 Eoptolink opened at <strong>CNY 185.99</strong>, fell to 179.61 and then climbed to <strong>189.70</strong> before closing at CNY 189.03 (+4.26%). Turnover of <strong>CNY 16.315bn</strong> was second in the market and its 7.04% turnover ratio was the highest in our group. Main funds bought CNY 934m net, including CNY 529m in the last half hour.

The catalyst was policy, not the company. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Cyberspace Administration and the NDRC published a next-generation network plan naming 400G/800G and 1.6T high-speed transport, ten-thousand-card cluster interconnection, 6G and ultra-high-speed optical links. At the same time CNY 2.567bn left semiconductors while CNY 4.527bn went into components and CNY 2.772bn into communications equipment. Zhongji Innolight rose 5.18% and TFC Optical was strong on the same flow.

The competitive picture is one leader and one strong second. Zhongji Innolight did CNY 27.769bn of Q3 revenue (+171.83%), CNY 8.587bn of net profit and a 46.61% gross margin, and leads Nvidia's 1.6T supply. Eoptolink is second at CNY 10.940bn; TFC Optical, a component supplier, did CNY 1.923bn. Q3 net profit across the three totals CNY 13.309bn, and the nine-month total of CNY 35.693bn is up 139.17% year on year. The difference is speed: Eoptolink's 80.30% Q3 growth is the slowest of the three and less than half of Zhongji Innolight's 171.83%. Through Q2 Eoptolink was growing faster; the floods and materials reversed the order in Q3.

The market itself keeps growing. LightCounting sees the 1.6T module market rising from USD 1.1bn in 2025 to USD 65.6bn in 2030 and overtaking 800G in 2027. CICC estimates 2026 demand at more than 40m 800G modules and more than 20m 1.6T modules. The bottleneck is the 3nm DSP: a Nomura expert put 2026 1.6T DSP demand above 20m units against shipments of 16-17m, with the shortage running into 2027. With 800G DSPs at USD 45-70 under long-term contracts and 1.6T at USD 120-150, the same unit volume doubles revenue.

Eoptolink's differentiation is LPO and silicon photonics. LPO removes the DSP and cuts power by more than 40%, and this company mass-produced it first. Silicon photonics is also taking a larger share. But the 1.6T mainstream is still pluggable modules, and its position behind Zhongji Innolight in Nvidia volumes will not change quickly. What it does have, the market understands, is custom work such as LRO for Meta.

Valuation has compressed. At <strong>18.09 times</strong> trailing earnings it trades below Zhongji Innolight's 19.56, and its maximum drawdown from the June peak is <strong>75%</strong>, the deepest of the three. Guotai Haitong set a CNY 322.25 target and 2026 EPS of CNY 12.89 on November 16; Tianfeng models net profit of CNY 19.115bn in 2026, CNY 37.463bn in 2027 and CNY 64.677bn in 2028. The catch is that the 2026 figure demands CNY 6.6bn in the fourth quarter alone.
