## Market

The Shanghai Composite closed at <strong>3,476.88 (+0.01%)</strong>, its first gain in two sessions, but it did not reclaim 3,500. The intraday range was 3,455.04 to 3,497.81, less than 34 points. The Shenzhen Component ended at <strong>10,084.04 (-0.02%)</strong>, ChiNext at <strong>2,189.82 (+0.29%)</strong>, the STAR 50 at <strong>1,169.99 (-1.36%)</strong>, the STAR Composite at <strong>1,304.37 (-1.00%)</strong> and the BSE 50 at <strong>752.44 (-0.45%)</strong>. The index level hides the damage: 1,766 advancers against 3,642 decliners, with 68 limit-ups against roughly five limit-downs. Turnover was CNY 1.3174tn, down CNY 65.7bn and lower for a third session; including Beijing the total was CNY 1.326tn.

New York could not hold its bounce. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell more than 7% intraday before closing down <strong>0.65%</strong>. Nvidia lost 4%, AMD 2% and Micron 4.5%, each down more than 8% at the worst point. October core PCE came in at <strong>3.3%</strong> year on year against 3.2% expected, the highest since November 2023, and the odds of a December hike moved to 100%. The Nasdaq fell 0.62%, the S&P 500 0.25% and the Dow rose 0.33%. Asia went the other way: the KOSPI rose <strong>0.29%</strong> to 4,711.21, clawing back part of Tuesday's 6.16% slide, with SK hynix up 2.23% and Samsung 0.35%. The Nikkei added 0.86%, and Kioxia jumped 12.57% after announcing work with Nvidia on using SSDs to expand GPU-accessible memory.

What moved A-shares was domestic. The Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, the Cyberspace Administration and the NDRC published an implementation plan for next-generation communications networks that names <strong>400G/800G and 1.6T high-speed transport</strong>, 6G, ultra-high-speed optical links and ten-thousand-card cluster interconnection. Communications equipment rose 2.74% and money moved into CPO and optical names. Industry news added to it: the Global Electronics Association warned that the PCB shortage will last <strong>into the first half of 2027</strong>, and TrendForce expects global AI server shipments to grow 28.3% in 2026. Printed circuit boards rose 4.37% and components 3.94%, with Jinbaize, Jinlu Electronics, Junya, Chongda and Aohong limit-up or up more than 15%. Advanced packaging also ran after Huawei's five-layer hybrid-bonded HBM paper appeared on the ISSCC 2027 agenda: Shenghe Jingwei rose 18.94%, and Kuaike Intelligent, Sanjia and Dagang were limit-up.

Chips were sold. Semiconductors saw CNY 2.567bn of net main-fund outflow, with equipment and materials weakest: AMEC -6.52%, Hwatsing -5.94%, NAURA -3.14%. That is why the STAR 50 fell 1.36% while ChiNext rose. SMIC lost 4.51% and the SMIC concept basket 0.71%. Money went to components (CNY 4.527bn inflow), communications equipment (CNY 2.772bn) and precious metals (CNY 1.19bn), while batteries and brokers saw outflows. Precious metals rose 4.23% as spot gold recovered from USD 3,426 an ounce to USD 3,588. Banks made new highs — Chengdu, Bank of Communications, CITIC and Jiangsu — and coal and oil supported the index. Large caps held the index up while growth fell: a rotation day.

Three of our eleven names rose. Zhongji Innolight closed at <strong>CNY 430.18 (+5.18%)</strong> after touching 440.00, taking both the largest turnover in the market (CNY 19.706bn) and the largest main-fund inflow (CNY 1.690bn). Eoptolink followed at <strong>CNY 189.03 (+4.26%)</strong>, and Shengyi Technology rose <strong>CNY 61.85 (+5.85%)</strong> with PCB and CCL names. Cambricon recovered <strong>CNY 942.85 (+3.85%)</strong>, about half of Tuesday's loss. On the other side, CXMT fell to <strong>CNY 32.69 (-2.71%)</strong> with CNY 795m of net main-fund selling, the worst in the market for a second day, and SMIC dropped to <strong>CNY 116.50 (-4.51%)</strong> after touching 115.76. Those two and the equipment names explain why the STAR 50 fell while ChiNext rose.
